## Coverage notes and gaps

- These archives intentionally preserve compact quoted/extracted passages, not full mirrors. Images, interactions, equations, note backlinks, and navigation chrome were omitted.
- Bret Victor's interactive examples are only represented by their explanatory prose; the local Markdown is sufficient for conceptual audit but not for replaying the interactions.
- Andy Matuschak's enabling-environment archive includes public working notes. The `§Enabling environments, games, and the Primer` note explicitly says it is outdated and points to a full essay at https://andymatuschak.org/primer; that essay was not archived here because the requested target files were limited to the three source files above plus this manifest.
- Several Andy links in the retained passages point to Patreon posts or other notes. Patron-only or otherwise inaccessible text was not archived or paraphrased.
- Linked public note titles are preserved as source-local backlinks where useful, but their target contents were not expanded unless the URL is listed in the table above.
- No pedagogical evidence is claimed beyond what appears in the retained source wording.
