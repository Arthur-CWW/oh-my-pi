## The casting register

**Body:** skinny, narrow-shouldered, flat/straight torso; long limbs or a compact boyish line. Avoid hourglass-first, cleavage-first, or maternal/soft-volume silhouettes.

**Face:** sharp eyes are more important than a sculpted angular jaw. Winged liner, narrow or half-lidded gaze, small mouth, and the ability to flip into a wide-eyed manic expression. “Cute” is useful only with a blade under it.

**Hair:** a silhouette carrier: long twin tails/braids, blunt long curtains with colored ends, or a short hard bob. Strong bangs, ahoge, horns/hat, or another one-read top shape. Generic shoulder-length salon hair loses.

**Energy:** tsundere/yandere voltage, mischievous menace, bratty challenge, and sudden manic expressiveness. The anchor cluster is Asuka + Yuno + Hu Tao + Power/Jinx, with Astolfo proving that androgyny/femboy softness is welcome. The companion should not read as a compliant idol, generic princess, or voluptuous gacha mascot.
