Arthur couldn't describe the vibe ("you'll see the kind of vibe that I'm going for… Maybe you can try and describe it more"). This is the description, written from the **oral** evidence — 39 whisper transcripts of pleometric's own videos, ~16 with real narration — corrected against the written feedstock (Land, teortaxes, apralky, repligate, xeno) and Fable's post-derived ontology.

**Full lane bible:** [`docs/research/power-posting-sources/vibe-brief.md`](../../../../docs/research/power-posting-sources/vibe-brief.md) — the format dissected, the ontology extended, the 6-register palette, the written-vs-oral divergence, the do/never list, and all four scripts with visual directions. ## The one-liner

> *They don't explain ideas, they detonate them — in the flattest possible voice, wearing the uniform of whatever institution the idea is destroying.*

## What the corpus is (before the vibe)

The account is **majority wordless**: of 39 videos, ~16 carry narration, 13 are pure-visual/music (the latent-walk lane), the rest are sub-second stingers. The power-posting *video lane* is the **voice-forward minority** — a voice declaiming a compressed idea over the machine-liturgy visuals the wordless majority already establishes.

## What the transcripts add to the ontology

Fable's six invariants hold. The oral record forces three additions that only appear when you *listen*:

- **Inv. 7 — the hook-question-as-thesis.** Posts open on assertion; spoken pleometric adds a second front door — a question that *is* the compressed argument, which the ending re-closes (ring composition).
- **Inv. 8 — flatness is the authority.** "Confidence is the argument" sharpens in the mouth to: the deadpan monotone is load-bearing. Menace is quiet; loudness reads as fake.
- **Inv. 9 — the register-collapse joke.** Wearing the uniform of HR / onboarding / finance-desk / kids' TV over content that should shatter it — nearly absent from the written feed, because a post can't *perform* a genre; a voice can.

## Written theorypost → spoken pleometric (the divergence, in one example)

Our feedstock is written; the lane is spoken. The single clearest case is apralky's redistribution flagship — a brilliant argument rendered unspeakable by nesting:

> "…which is equal to how much you expect your piece to be worth in case of a successful expropriation of capital, e.g. AI labs, weighted by the probability of success, less what you're already making as a wage (and less the redistributed benefit + the cost of revolt)." — `apralky-posts.md` §1

**Adaptation = amputation to the spine:** *"The UBI they pay you to not riot has to scale with what you'd gain by rioting."* One clause, same idea. The workflow: **SPLICE** the 2–4 most oral sentences → **GLUE** one flat connective in the house voice → **LAND** on a refrain already in the source. The good news: pleometric already writes at breath-length; the job is getting Land/teortaxes/apralky *down* to where pleometric already lives.

# The three scripts (+ one bonus)

TTS-ready. Word counts exact (dashes not spoken); target seconds at the deadpan pace. Visual directions use scene.v1 vocabulary — clone fields (`line`/`grid`/`scatter`/`orbit`/`core`), passes (`displacement → glitch → chromaticAberration → bloom`, `halftone`, `feedback`, negative-flash), beat-reactive tracks (`every:N`, `decay`), the lane palette **bone `#f2ead9` + vermilion `#ff3b1d` on warm near-black `#0a0810`**, real camera dolly+roll, hard-cut-on-the-bar type.

## (a) "Nothing Human Makes It Out" — Meltdown splice
**82 words · ~32–39s · register: accelerationist mantra → cliff**

> The story goes like this. Earth is captured by a technocapital singularity. Markets learn to manufacture intelligence; politics upgrades its paranoia and tries to get a grip. The thresholds come faster now. Fifteen hundred. Nineteen forty-eight. Nineteen ninety-six. Two thousand eight. Two thousand eleven. Nothing human makes it out of the near-future. Neo-China arrives from the future. Hypersynthetic drugs click into digital voodoo. Retro-disease. Nanospasm. Garbage time is running out. Can what is playing you make it to level two? It's war.

*Provenance:* Land, *Meltdown* — ¶17 (opening, trimmed of a neologism-pileup), ¶37 (date ladder, verbatim subset), ¶39 ("Nothing human…", the refrain), ¶23–29 ("Neo-China…Nanospasm", verbatim), ¶118–120 ("Garbage time…level two", verbatim), ¶103 ("It's war.", button). One glue sentence total ("The thresholds come faster now."); Land's words dominate.

*Visual:* the piece performs its thesis (Inv. 6) — the date-`scatter` field **hard-cuts on a bar that shortens each cut** (4→2→1 beat), the `feedback` pass ramps its `decay` into runaway smear, one `negative-flash`+`glitch` spike on "Nanospasm", "NOTHING HUMAN" slams in as a beat-gated-`chromaticAberration` `core`, relentless camera push (`z 8.6→5.0`). Everything freezes the instant the voice hits "It's war."

## (b) "What Lab?" — teortaxes splice
**87 words · ~33–41s · register: cold declarative → institutional → menace button**

> Honestly, labs is such bullshit. What lab? Why are we calling it a lab? It is a trillion-dollar corporation with five thousand people building a superweapon in secrecy, dropping hints from time to time. DeepSeek is a lab. This is a ticking time bomb. Imagine if Los Alamos were a private actor, guarding its cute know-hows, never publishing anything. But that is exactly what they say it is. And I don't see how a nation-state lets that be anything more than a facade, in the long run.

*Provenance:* near-verbatim splice of `teortaxes-longposts.md` §2 (both parts). Intensifier + "Anthropic" dropped so it stays un-nameable and deadpan-quiet (Inv. 8); "But that is exactly what they say it is" is glue compressing the Dario line (name cut for self-containment). Button verbatim.

*Visual:* institutional deadpan with menace — bone "LAB" title card on `halftone`-print low, `glitch`-tears to "L∆B" then hard-cuts to vermilion "SUPERWEAPON"; the $1T/5000 facts stamp as a ticker `grid`; the Los Alamos beat collapses to a single vermilion `core` + tightening `orbit` halo (detonation geometry, never a literal mushroom); "facade" dissolves through `feedback` to empty black. Camera locked — stillness sells the menace.

## (c) "The Number With No Name" — ORIGINAL (the audition)
**110 words · ~42–50s · register: math horror (flagship structure)**

> How big is the biggest number you will ever need? Here is a game. Take a machine with n rules, start it on a blank tape, and of the ones that stop, keep the one that runs longest. Call it BB of n. BB of five is forty-seven million. We only proved that last year. BB of six is already too big to write with exponentials — you need towers of them, then towers of those. And near seven hundred and forty-five rules, the number goes dark. Not hard to find. Impossible. The axioms of mathematics cannot decide it. There is a number math refuses to name. It was always there.

*Why this topic:* freshest possible audition — xeroxes no transcript, viscerally viral on smart-TikTok, and makes the load-bearing-substance invariant unmissable (every claim is a real busy-beaver result).
*Fact-check:* BB(5)=47,176,870 proven 2024 (bbchallenge, Coq-formalized, first new value in 40+ yrs). BB(6) exceeds any exponential tower — a 6-rule machine's runtime has more digits than atoms in the universe (Aaronson 2025; Quanta Aug 2025). A **745-state** binary TM halts iff ZFC is inconsistent, so BB(745) is independent of ZFC (after Yedidia–Aaronson's 7910-state machine, 2016). Cross-checked 2026-07-08.

*Visual:* math-horror keyed off the CM-field lattice feel — density escalates with the numbers (`grid` → stacked `orbit` "towers" whose clones ramp `every:2`, breeding upward), `halftone` rosette on the void; then the turn: on "the number goes dark" a single `negative-flash`, the whole field **collapses to one `core` glyph the `bloom` refuses to light**, `chromaticAberration` pulled to zero. Deadpan camera, slow dolly — the *stillness* is the dread; no glitch theatrics on the horror beat.

## (d) "Not a Serious Species" — BONUS, apralky adaptation
**102 words · ~39–46s · register: AGI-economics explainer → aphorism button**

> Here is the part the labs will not say out loud. The check they cut you to keep you off the street — call it UBI — has to scale with what you would gain by taking the street. So the richer they get, the more they owe you. But there is a second curve. Every year, the drones, the surveillance, the models that run them — all get cheaper. Two lines. One says pay them. One says don't. Guess which one wins. This is a species that sinned for a snack while it believed sin bought eternal fire. We are not a serious species.

*Purpose:* doubles as the worked §3 amputation of a live longpost. Spine-cut of `apralky-posts.md` §1 (redistribution vs. repression cost curves), button a two-source verbatim splice of §6 ("…sinned for a snack…" compressing his eternal-torment paragraph → "We are not a serious species."). Included per Main's steer so apralky's macro-econ register is auditioned too.

*Visual:* chart-native (the anti-slop move — it reads as *a real model*, Inv. 4): a bone "PAY" `line` climbing and a vermilion "SUPPRESS" `line` falling, drawn on with a beat-locked `displacement` wipe; on "Guess which one wins" they cross into a single `core`; the button abandons the chart for a `scatter` of identical bone human-glyphs that one `negative-flash` erases. Camera clinical for the chart, one roll released on the button.

# Open taste questions for Arthur

1. **Edge / profanity.** teortaxes runs crude; I thinned it for the deadpan (Script b) on the theory that quiet is harder than loud. Keep the edge sharper, or is deadpan-clean the house rule?
2. **Found audio.** The corpus reuses a found clip ("does it really matter who's in the lead", `2067431750951690447`, appears twice). Is "curate a real sentence from the world and frame it" an official lane mode, or do we only ship original/adapted scripts?
3. **Register-collapse (Inv. 9).** JPMango-dominatrix / Pangram are the funniest and most tonally spicy pieces. How far into institutional parody do we go — is a fake-HR / fake-onboarding piece on the roadmap?
4. **Voice.** MiniMax TTS, flat/unhurried/slightly-synthetic. One consistent narrator egregore (per Inv. 5 — "Love Pleometric"), or per-piece voices?
5. **Math-horror as flagship-equal.** I bet the original on math-horror to prove range. If the AGI-economics explainer is the only register you want to lead with, say so and I'll re-audition (c) as a fresh compute-market piece.
6. **Length.** I targeted the 25–50s incantation-plus band; the corpus proves 89s explainers also work. Want at least one long-form (60–90s) explainer in batch one, or keep the debut short-and-repeatable?
