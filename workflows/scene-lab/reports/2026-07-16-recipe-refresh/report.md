## New recipes

### 11. Simulation Workbench

The full corpus makes live cause-and-effect a repeatable presentation grammar rather than a subject category: visible controls, gestures, parameters, or constraints produce an immediate system response. It qualifies with five exemplars across two handles: SkyeSharkie (`2072508957583962162-1`, `2073294937601503349-1`) and poetengineer__ (`2032138243399917658-1`, `2041553085554663643-1`, `2062652547093967019-1`). Its `scene.v1` mapping stays within staged video, timed callouts, and input/result cuts; live simulation and interaction are named gaps.

### 12. Morphic State Passage

The expanded catalog supports continuous transformation as its own grammar, distinct from the abrupt Hard-Button Loop and the traveling Feedback Corridor: source and target remain legible while voxels, particles, fields, tessellations, or material morphs carry the passage. It qualifies with five exemplars across three handles: SkyeSharkie (`2072697701666574404-1`), poetengineer__ (`2023050668030750779-1`, `2054111366672273911-1`, `2058808511807578357-1`), and abelian_soup (`2014699246415683803-1`). Native morphing remains a named renderer gap; staged `videoFrames` is the documented approximation.

## Grammar revisions

Nine evidence-driven edits carry the required `(rev. 2026-07-16)` tag:

- **Terminal Reliquary:** extended the carrier from authored diagram plates to live vector/wireframe telemetry and oscilloscope motion when it reads as instrument output.
- **Reference-Pile Retention Stack:** added sequential active-panel grids and a moving-logo anchor; named gameplay/faces/hands/continuous body motion as kinesis between accents.
- **Typographic Incantation:** added dynamic reflow around moving forms, negative-space masking, and data-driven type morphs without relaxing legibility.
- **Mascot Clone Chorus:** named faces, hand signs, and repeated body gestures as continuous kinesis; beats may reorganize rather than generate the motion.
- **Found-Clip Evidence Wall:** added restrained shot–reverse-shot and multi-angle evidence; named face/hand/gesture motion as kinesis.
- **Feedback Corridor Pilgrimage:** named continuous travel itself as kinesis and beat sync as one modulation species.
- **Hard-Button Loop:** named readable body/hand/face motion as setup kinesis whose interruption gives the hard event force.

Chrome Halo Procession, Institutional Signal Hijack, and Retro-Web Urgency Shrine gained exemplars but no grammar edits because the fuller evidence reinforced rather than contradicted their existing grammar. Simulation Workbench and Morphic State Passage include kinesis in their initial definitions and therefore do not use revision tags.
