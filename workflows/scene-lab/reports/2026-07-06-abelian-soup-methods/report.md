## Key findings

- @abelian_soup and @pleometric are separate public accounts. Abel's work is visibly influenced by Pleometric; he calls his own system "pleorama slopcore" and says his aim was "to move away from just blatantly ripping off your format lol". @pleometric advises him to constrain style guides.
- The core system is an LLM-agent-driven offline/batch harness: Claude/Opus gets a topic prompt, then composes script, scenes, audio/video generation, effect compositions, and layering from a prebuilt effect/tool menu.
- The richest concrete technical artifact is Abel's linked `https://github.com/abeliansoup/latwalk` repo: CLIP/DINO/color-histogram image ordering, beat/onset timing, pulse/glitch/rave effects, kinetic noise, negative flashes, FILM interpolation, timing audits, and reusable `latmatch/` modules.
- The public posts disclose architecture and toolchain more than shader source. Effects are named, but the private Mar 16 harness internals are not public.
