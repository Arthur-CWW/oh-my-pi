# Xi Files AV Production Plan

Purpose: turn `xi-files-video-essay.md` into an editor-ready production plan for images, video, music, sound, and graphics.

Style target: investigative video essay with cinematic B-roll, document-driven motion graphics, maps, sharp text cards, and music-led emotional turns. Think Johnny Harris workflow inspiration, not a clone: research-first, visually dense, map/document-forward, and organized by story beats.

## Research Basis

- Johnny Harris describes his visual style as a blend of motion graphics and cinematic videography used to explain complex issues in relatable ways: https://www.johnnyharris.ch/about
- His researcher/writer call emphasizes sorting through complex data, archives, and journals, then condensing them into a digestible video script: https://www.johnnyharris.ch/researcher-writer-application
- His public visual storytelling course guide describes organizing footage by where it goes in the story, building a "selects" sequence, using labels/adjustment layers, and treating music as a major structural tool. It groups music into "thinky", "feely", and "fun" categories and suggests gathering many tracks before the edit: https://downloads.ctfassets.net/bhsr3r63z25m/63ngEHICdN0e6axVZU2W4d/9c0efff36556fd55d43af63b366d2c97/Visual_Storytelling_Course_Guide.pdf
- Documentary AV scripts commonly use a two-column format: left side is what the viewer sees, right side is what the viewer hears. Good references: https://guides.library.georgetown.edu/documentary and https://blog.celtx.com/essential-av-script-template-for-better-video-scripts/

## Notation Legend

Use these tags in the editing script.

- `[DOC]`: document screenshot, article text, legal language, highlighting, cursor zoom, red underline.
- `[MAP]`: animated map, locator, boundary/context graphic.
- `[BROLL]`: filmed or stock footage.
- `[ARCHIVE]`: historical photo, old map, public-domain image, scanned book/document.
- `[GFX]`: motion graphic, diagram, typography, timeline, stamp animation.
- `[A-ROLL]`: narrator on camera, if used.
- `[TEXT]`: words shown on screen.
- `[MUSIC: THINKY]`: neutral, investigative, lightly pulsing, good for explanation/history.
- `[MUSIC: FEELY]`: emotional, spacious, reflective, good for stakes/ending.
- `[MUSIC: FUN]`: lighter, mischievous, irony/comedy/absurdity.
- `[SFX]`: designed sound effect, not naturally in the shot.
- `[NAT]`: natural sound or ambience from footage.
- `[HIT]`: sharp music/SFX accent on a word, cut, title, or reveal.
- `[DROP]`: music or ambience suddenly cuts out.
- `[J-CUT]`: next audio starts before next image.
- `[L-CUT]`: previous audio continues under next image.
- `[SELECTS]`: candidate footage to pull into the selects timeline before the real edit.

## Music Palette

Do not pick one bed and loop it for 15 minutes. Music should mark story state changes.

- Cold Open: `FUN + THINKY`, curious pulse, slightly absurd. Tempo 95-115 BPM. Add dead stops.
- Intro: `THINKY`, calm investigative arpeggio or muted piano pulse. Tempo 80-100 BPM.
- Chapter 1: `FUN`, light bureaucratic absurdity. Dry percussion, pizzicato, muted synth ticks.
- Chapter 2: `THINKY DARK`, lower pulse, more tension, no melodrama.
- Chapter 3: `THINKY`, procedural search energy. Ticking percussion and soft bass.
- Chapter 4: `FEELY + CINEMATIC`, slow ceremonial tension, low strings, bell-like tones.
- Chapter 5: `THINKY`, institutional and analytical. Neutral, sparse, steady.
- Chapter 6: `THINKY DARK`, legal/enforcement tension. Sub bass, sparse hits, little melody.
- Chapter 7: `FEELY`, reflective authority question. Warm pads, piano, restrained swell.
- Ending: `FEELY RESOLUTION`, not triumphant. Let the final question hang.

## Sound Design Palette

Use sound as punctuation, not wallpaper.

- Paper shuffle: every document reveal or application joke.
- Stamp hit: "approved", "denied", "unauthorized", "Order No. 5".
- Pen scratch: legal detail moments.
- Clock tick: cold open and search-team chapter.
- Reverse swell: before each article reveal.
- Low boom: after "Applications. For reincarnation."
- Prayer bell: only for chapter transitions into religious/ritual material.
- Map pin tap: search-team and legitimacy sections.
- Comment wall glitch: when describing internet outrage.
- Room tone: government/committee B-roll.
- Silence/drop: after the most absurd lines so the joke lands.

## Editor's Selects Pass

Before cutting the main timeline:

1. Build a `SELECTS_DOCUMENTS` sequence with every source-document crop and text highlight.
2. Build a `SELECTS_MAPS` sequence with locator map, old map, and search-board graphics.
3. Build a `SELECTS_MONASTERY` sequence with usable temple/prayer/detail shots.
4. Build a `SELECTS_GOVERNMENT` sequence with paperwork, offices, stamps, corridors, meeting rooms.
5. Build a `SELECTS_MUSIC` sequence with 20-30 candidate tracks grouped as thinky, feely, and fun.
6. Build a `SELECTS_SFX` sequence with the recurring stamp, paper, pen, clock, bell, boom, and reverse-swell cues.

## Licensing Notes

- Do not rip footage, music, or sound from Johnny Harris videos.
- Use the supplied Xi Files screenshot as source material and recreate it in motion graphics for clarity.
- Prefer public-domain/Creative Commons materials only after checking each file license and attribution requirements.
- For stock/music/SFX, use a licensed library available to the project and keep receipts/attribution in `01_SOURCE_DOCS`.
- For generated visuals, label them internally as recreations/illustrations, not archival evidence.
