# Xi Files Video Essay Draft

Source basis: the supplied "Xi Files" image/text is the Wikipedia article for "State Religious Affairs Bureau Order No. 5", officially named "Measures on the Management of the Reincarnation of Living Buddhas in Tibetan Buddhism." This draft uses only that supplied text and separates documented claims from interpretation.

## Source Notes

- Passed during a State Administration for Religious Affairs conference on July 13, 2007; implemented September 1, 2007.
- Requires a Reincarnation Application from Buddhist temples in China before recognizing individuals as tulkus, or reincarnated teachers.
- Article 2 frames reincarnation around state unification, minority unity, religious concord, social harmony, the normal order of Tibetan Buddhism, and non-interference by foreign organizations or individuals.
- Article 4 lists conditions where applicants to be reincarnating living Buddhas may not be reincarnated.
- Article 7 describes approval, a reincarnation guidance team, and a search team for the reincarnate soul child.
- Article 8 preserves the golden urn process for living Buddhas historically recognized by drawing lots from it.
- Article 11 creates administrative sanctions for unauthorized reincarnation affairs, with criminal responsibility pursued where a crime has been constituted.
