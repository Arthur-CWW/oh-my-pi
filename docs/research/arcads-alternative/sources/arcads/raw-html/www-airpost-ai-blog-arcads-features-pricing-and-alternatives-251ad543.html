Arcads: Features, Pricing, and 5 Alternatives

Arcads promises fast UGC-style ads without hiring creators. For many marketers, that’s enough reason to check it out. But like most AI tools, the experience depends on what you actually need: volume, editing flexibility, product demos, or full-funnel creative support.

In this blog, you'll find out what Arcads is, who it’s best for, the tradeoffs to expect, and some alternatives to Arcads that might be a better fit for you. 

What is Arcads

Arcads is an AI ad generator focused on TikTok-style UGC videos. Instead of hiring creators, you work with a library of 1,000+ AI “actors” or generate your own avatar, then script short performance ads for mobile feeds. Teams use it mainly for direct-response creatives for apps, SaaS, and DTC products where you want a talking head, social proof, and fast iteration, without running a full creator program


Key features

  • Library of 1,000+ AI actors designed to look and perform like UGC creators.

  • Option to create your own AI actor that can hold your product, show your app, or wear your brand. 

  • AI ad generator for short social videos, with in-tool examples tied to real campaign metrics like views and revenue lift. 

  • Text-to-speech and lip-sync so actors can deliver your script in a natural way. 

  • Localization into 30+ languages for running the same creative concept across markets. 

  • Ability to attach multiple actors to the same script and generate several variations in one run, useful for testing or client options.

  • Built-in hook generator to spark ideas for opening lines before you write the full script.

Pricing

Arcads doesn’t list pricing publicly. Plans appear after onboarding. But a typical tiers look like:

  • The starter plan is around $110/month. You can generate up to 10 videos per month.

  • The Creator plan starts from $220/month, giving you up to 20 videos per month.

  • You can also get custom plans if you plan to generate a large volume of ads and need access to APIs. 

Plans are structured around how many finished videos you need each month, so you can compare the per-video cost directly with hiring UGC creators or influencers.

Why People Look for Arcads Alternatives

Marketers generally like Arcads for how real the actors look, but once they start using it, a few practical gaps show up. These explain why some teams keep a second tool alongside Arcads or switch entirely.

1. It doesn’t cover the full ad workflow

Arcads is great at generating the “person talking to camera” part, but everything around that still needs manual work. You write your own script, export the clip, and then finish it in CapCut, Premiere, or whatever editing tool you use. For teams that want a single platform that handles scripting, visuals, captions, overlays, and final delivery, this feels incomplete.

2. Limited use for physical products

Because the actors don’t actually interact with real items, Arcads is stronger for digital products. Ecommerce brands can still use it for hooks or voiceovers, but the main part of the ad — the demo — has to happen elsewhere. That’s usually where tools like Creatify or Airpost feel better suited.

3. Not affordable if you need volume

The output is high quality, but the pricing is based on how many finished videos you generate. If you’re running lots of tests or need multiple angles per script, credits run out quickly. Competitors with unlimited or higher-volume plans sometimes make more sense for testing-heavy teams.

4. No built-in script tools or templates

Arcads assumes you already know what you want the actor to say. There’s no idea generator (aside from a basic hook tool), no script templates, and no campaign structure help. If you want a system that helps with concept → script → edit → export, you typically look elsewhere.

5. Still early on editing features

The core actors look real, but the platform has very little editing control today. You can’t add pacing notes, cuts, transitions, or overlays inside Arcads, and small adjustments (tone, timing, gestures) require re-generating the entire video. Marketers who want more creative control often shift to tools with deeper editing layers.

Alternatives to Arcads

If Arcads feels close but not quite right for your workflow, you’re not alone. Different teams need different levels of control, speed, editing, or product showcasing and that’s where other tools start to make more sense. 

Below, we’ll look at a few options that are good alternatives to Arcads. 

Airpost

Airpost is a strong Arcads alternative if you care less about AI actors and more about getting high-performing ad creatives at scale. Instead of asking you to script, direct, and refine everything yourself, Airpost uses a hybrid model: AI does the heavy lifting, and their creative strategists guide the angles, pacing, and hooks so the ads are built for real paid social performance. 

It’s closer to having a fast, on-demand creative team than a self-serve AI avatar generator. You can browse real examples of ads created with Airpost here.

Key Features

  • Blends your assets, Airpost’s asset library, and AI-generated footage into new ad concepts

  • “Living brief” that updates ads automatically when messaging, personas, or offers change

  • 10–100+ new ads per week depending on your plan

  • Creative strategists who refine concepts, scripts, visuals, and compliance

  • Hands-off uploads to Meta/TikTok following your naming conventions

  • Always-on monitoring to spin new variations from your top performers


Pricing

Airpost pricing starts at $8,000/month or 4% of spend on Airpost-touched ads (whichever is higher). This includes the platform, creative team, editing, compliance, and weekly ad volume across formats like 4:5 and 9:16.

Zeely AI

Zeely AI is another option if you want a mobile-first tool that can generate quick UGC-style ads and run basic campaigns without needing separate apps or freelancers. It’s built with small businesses and solo e-commerce owners in mind — people who want to launch ads fast, test ideas cheaply, and manage everything from a phone. The workflow feels very “all-in-one”: you paste a product URL, Zeely pulls the data, generates a script, creates a talking-head video or image ad, and helps push it into a Meta/TikTok campaign.


Key Features

  • UGC video generator using 30+ AI avatars based on real creators

  • Auto-scripts from URLs + quick editing for hooks, captions, and background music

  • Image ads built from 200+ templates, sized for all major platforms

  • Campaign automation (launch, test, adjust across Meta/TikTok)

  • E-commerce tools like no-code landing pages and simple order tracking

  • Recent models (Veo 3.1, Kling 2.6) for more natural motion and cleaner UGC-style footage

Pricing

Zeely uses a subscription + credits model.

  • Plans typically start around $25–$35/month for light usage

  • Higher tiers (around $79/month+) unlock more credits, advanced generation, and campaign automation

  • Credits are used for each video, and add-ons available for volume work.

  • Trials exist, but require upfront payment, and cancellations must go through support

Exact pricing varies by region and feature set, so check their website for current rates.

Creatify

Creatify is an AI ad generator built for teams that want to turn product pages, images, or short briefs into ready-to-run UGC-style ads. Instead of relying on a single avatar model, Creatify combines URL scraping, AI scripting, avatars, and product-shot generation to produce 5–10 ad variations in one go. It works well for e-commerce brands that need volume — quick tests, multiple hooks, new angles — without coordinating creators or setting up shoots.


Key Features

  • URL-to-Ad generation: paste a product link, get multiple ads automatically

  • AI UGC Avatars (1,500+ options) with gestures, emotions, and multilingual voices

  • Product Video Generator for turning static images into animated product shots

  • AdMax suite for trend discovery, competitor insights, and structured creative testing

  • Batch mode for bulk asset generation across multiple SKUs

  • Platform-ready exports for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Snap

The system is built for generating many variations fast, rather than fine-grained editing or cinematic control.

Pricing

Creatify uses a credit-based model, with features scaling by plan:

  • Free – $0. You get 10 credits/month, watermark exports, and basic AI video tools.

  • Starter – $19/month (1,200 credits/year). This includes watermark removal, AI video ads, product videos, 100+ avatars, 29 languages, and videos up to 2 minutes.

  • Pro – $49/month. With this, you get 1,500+ avatars, 3 custom avatars, stronger video models (e.g., Sora 2 Pro, Veo 3.1), 10-minute videos, product video styles, and Meta/TikTok publishing.

  • Custom plan includes AdMax (research + optimization), bulk creation, templates, multi-brand support, API volume discounts, and done-for-you creative services.

Credits do not roll over, so teams generating high volumes typically choose annual plans for better value.

MagicUGC

MagicUGC is an AI UGC video generator focused on producing short, influencer-style ads using AI actors, preset scenes, and auto-structured scripts. It positions itself as a faster substitute for hiring creators, especially for brands that want TikTok-native, testimonial-style videos without managing freelancers. Compared to Arcads, MagicUGC leans heavily on templates, prebuilt “personas,” and automated scene assembly rather than manual scripting and multi-take control.

Key Features

MagicUGC is built around speed and predictability rather than fine editing:

  • AI UGC Actors (1,000+ options) with adjustable tone, pacing, and emotion

  • Auto Scene Generation that stitches multiple clips into a full ad

  • AI Script Optimization that rewrites your rough draft into platform-ready ad copy

  • Persona Targeting, for example, you can select actors, tone, and pacing based on the audience segment

  • Multi-language support across ~30 languages

  • Analytics dashboard showing predictive performance scores and engagement estimates

  • Cultural and demographic adaptation for localized ad variants

It’s designed to create dozens of variations quickly, making it useful for brands testing hooks or running high-volume creative experiments.

Pricing

MagicUGC offers three paid tiers with a short free trial (you can generate two videos before committing). Plans are structured around monthly video limits and access to different avatar libraries.

  • Basic ($149/mo yearly) gives you up to 20 videos each month with 2 basic avatars and core features like multilingual output, cultural adaptation, and performance analytics. 

  • Pro ($179/mo yearly) increases the limit to 50 videos, unlocks 5 expanded avatars, adds script optimization, persona targeting, and brand-style controls. 

  • Turbo ($249/mo yearly) removes caps entirely with unlimited videos, up to 10 premium avatars, a dedicated success manager, and access to advanced editing and optimization tools

Across all plans, credits don’t roll over, but generation is unlimited—credits only apply to exporting videos. The year-over-year savings and free trial make it easier to test, but ongoing costs can add up for brands that aren’t actively producing volume each month.

Pick the tool that moves your metrics, not just your workflow

Arcads is great when you need talking-head UGC fast. But choosing an ad generator isn’t really about avatars or interfaces. It’s about how much creative output you can ship, how quickly you can test new angles, and whether the tool actually helps you hit your performance goals.

Some teams want the lowest lift. Some want more control. Some only need a few videos a month. And some need a pipeline that keeps producing new variations without them babysitting every step.

If you fall into that last group of people who're running real budgets, testing weekly, and needing creatives that keep up with your strategy, Airpost is usually the next tool worth exploring. It blends AI with a creative team, updates ads when your messaging shifts, and keeps a steady stream of variations running without adding more work to your plate.
