You can now translate any video into multiple languages with **natural voice-over, automatic dubbing, and precise lipsync** — all in just a few clicks.

This feature is designed for **international creators, global brands, agencies**, and anyone who needs to scale multilingual video content efficiently.

## **Key features**

- **Upload any video** and get a fully translated version with a natural-sounding voice-over
- **Automatic lipsync**, adapted to the target language for seamless results
- **One-click bulk translation** into multiple languages
- **Supports 130+ languages and accents** for global distribution

No manual editing, no re-recording, no complex setup. **Note:** The "Translate video" tool has a file size limit of 100 MB for input files. Ensure your video files meet this requirement before proceeding.

## **👇 How to use it**

1. Open the **composer**
2. Click on **“See more”**
3. Select **“Video Translation”**
4. Upload your video
5. Choose your target language(s)
6. Click **Generate**

## **🤔 Tips for best results**

To maximize translation accuracy and lipsync quality:

- Use **clear videos** with **clean, high-quality audio**
- Avoid strong background noise or overlapping voices
- Ensure the main speaker is clearly visible on screen
- Ensure your video file size is under 100 MB to meet tool requirements

The better the source video, the better the final translated output.

## **Use cases**

- Localizing ads for international markets
- Translating UGC or creator content at scale
- Repurposing high-performing videos across regions
- Expanding brand reach without re-shooting content

# Limitations and Constraints

- The "Translate video" tool has a file size limit of 100 MB for input files.
- Translation and transcription features are not available through the API; these functionalities must be accessed via the built-in tools.
