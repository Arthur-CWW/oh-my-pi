## **Overview**

**Replace Actor** lets you seamlessly swap the subject in an existing video with a new character—without rebuilding the video from scratch.

This feature is ideal for reusing winning creatives while adapting them to different audiences, markets, or branding needs.

## **What Is Replace Actor?**

**Replace Actor** is a tool within **Arcads** that allows you to replace the main character in a video while preserving:

- the original scene
- camera movement
- timing and pacing
- overall visual structure
- Script and voice

You simply provide a video template and an image of the new character, and Arcads handles the transformation.

## **👇 How to Use Replace Actor**

1. Select **Replace Actor** from the tool list
2. Upload a **video template**

   - Accepted format: .mp4
   - Maximum size: 150 MB
3. Upload an **image of the character to replace the original actor**

   - Accepted formats: .jpg, .png
   - Maximum size: 50 MB
4. Generate — Arcads will automatically replace the actor in the video

## **Best Practices**

- Use a **clear, front-facing image** of the replacement character
- Ensure the face is well-lit and not obstructed
- Avoid extreme angles or heavy motion in the source video
- Videos with a clearly visible subject deliver the best results
