## **Overview**

**Fashion Try-On** allows you to instantly visualize your clothing on real-looking models without photoshoots, prompting, or technical setup.

Simply upload images of your clothing, select a template, and Arcads automatically generates realistic visuals of people wearing your brand’s apparel.

This tool is designed for fashion brands that want to move fast, test creatives, and showcase products at scale.

## **What Is Fashion Try-On?**

**Fashion Try-On** is a feature within **Arcads** that lets you place your clothing onto AI-generated models in just a few clicks.

No prompts are required.

No styling or posing instructions needed.

The system automatically adapts:

- fit and proportions
- posture and pose
- lighting and fabric appearance

to deliver natural, high-quality results.
