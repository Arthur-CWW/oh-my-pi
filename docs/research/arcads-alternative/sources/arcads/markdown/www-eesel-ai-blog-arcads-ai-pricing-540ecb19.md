## What is Arcads AI?

So, what’s the deal with Arcads AI? In a nutshell, it’s a [platform that turns your text scripts into video ads](https://www.arcads.ai/) starring realistic AI actors. The idea is to skip the hassle of hiring creators, organizing video shoots, and sitting through long editing sessions. Instead, you write a script, pick from a library of over 300 AI actors, and get a talking-head video pretty quickly.

It's built mainly for performance marketing teams, e-commerce brands, and anyone selling digital products like SaaS, courses, or apps. The main selling point is saving time and money, letting teams A/B test a ton of ad creatives without the high cost of traditional video production. The AI actors are based on real people, which helps the final videos feel more authentic than some of the other AI avatar tools out there.

## Arcads AI pricing: A deep dive into core features

Before we talk numbers, let’s look at what you’re actually paying for. Arcads is pretty focused on doing one thing well: creating that core video of a person talking to the camera. Here are the main features.

### 300+ realistic AI actors

The big draw here is the huge library of AI actors. These aren’t clunky avatars; they’re generated from footage of real, consenting people, which makes their expressions and movements feel surprisingly natural. The library has a [good mix of ages, ethnicities, and settings](https://www.arcads.ai/features/ai-video-generator) (like an office, home, or outdoors), so you can find an actor that matches the audience you’re trying to reach.

### Text-to-speech and speech-to-speech capabilities

The platform’s engine runs on text-to-speech. You give it a script, and the AI actor performs it with their lips synced to the words. The punctuation you use matters, too. A question mark or exclamation point will change the actor's tone. Arcads also has a [speech-to-speech feature](https://dupple.com/tools/arcads-ai) where you can upload a recording of your own voice. The platform then maps your tone and cadence onto the AI actor for a more personalized delivery.

### Bulk creation for A/B testing

For any marketer who relies heavily on A/B testing, this is a handy feature. You can take one script and see what it looks like with dozens of different actors at the same time. This lets you quickly create a bunch of ad variations to test which person connects best with your audience on platforms like Meta, TikTok, or Instagram.

## Known limitations to consider

While Arcads is good at creating the initial video clip, it’s not a one-stop shop. Getting an ad ready for a campaign often means using other tools and doing some manual work, which can add hidden costs and slow things down.

### No built-in video editor

This is a big one. Arcads gives you a clean talking-head video, but you can't add text overlays, captions, background music, or any B-roll footage inside the platform. To finish your ad, you have to download the video and bring it into a separate editor like CapCut or Adobe Premiere Pro. This [adds more steps to your process](https://superscale.ai/compare/superscale-vs-arcads) and means you need to be comfortable using other software.

### You need to provide your own scripts

Arcads doesn’t have an AI scriptwriting tool. They offer a separate "hook generator," but you’re on your own for writing the full ad script. You’ll either have to write it yourself or use another AI tool like ChatGPT. This means the quality of your ad's message really depends on your own copywriting skills.

### Advanced features are locked behind expensive plans

Things like getting access to premium voices from ElevenLabs or the ability to clone your own actors aren't available on the standard plans, which limits how much you can customize your videos.

This means your process ends up looking something like this: you write a script in one tool, generate the talking-head video in Arcads, download that file, then upload it into *another* editing tool to add captions, B-roll, and music before you can finally get it on an ad platform.

### Is the Arcads AI pricing worth it?

At $11 per video on the paid plans, Arcads is way [cheaper than hiring a human UGC creator](https://creatify.ai/review/arcads-ai), who could charge anywhere from $80 to over $200 for just one ad. From a pure cost standpoint, the math works out, especially if you need to produce a lot of creative assets.

But the value starts to look a little different when you factor in the workflow gaps. The subscription cost doesn't cover the time you'll spend in other video editors or the potential cost of that software. ## Frequently asked questions

Can you explain the different tiers of Arcads AI pricing and what features come with each?

The blog details Starter, Creator, and Pro plans. The Starter and Creator plans offer a set number of videos monthly with core AI actors, while the Pro plan is custom and includes team access, API access, and ElevenLabs integration.

Is there a free trial available to test Arcads AI before committing to a subscription?

Currently, Arcads AI does not offer a free trial. You must subscribe to a paid plan to try out the platform and its features.

What are the main benefits that justify the Arcads AI pricing for marketers?

The primary benefit is the significantly lower cost per video ($11) compared to hiring human UGC creators (who charge $80-$200+). This allows marketers to produce and A/B test a large volume of video creatives quickly and cost-effectively.

Are there any additional costs or tools I need to consider beyond the Arcads AI pricing?

Yes, Arcads AI doesn't include a built-in video editor or scriptwriting tool. You'll need separate software (like CapCut or Adobe Premiere Pro) for editing, adding captions, music, and B-roll, which can add to your overall workflow time and potentially costs.

How does the Arcads AI pricing structure affect access to advanced features like custom voices or personal actor cloning?

Based on current information, many advanced features, such as premium voices from ElevenLabs or the ability to clone your own actors, are typically reserved for the higher-tier, custom Arcads AI pricing plans, not the standard subscriptions.

What kind of return on investment can one expect from Arcads AI pricing, particularly for A/B testing?

The platform enables rapid bulk creation of diverse ad variations, allowing marketers to quickly test different actors and messages. While the subscription cost doesn't cover external editing time, the low cost per video facilitates extensive A/B testing to optimize ad performance efficiently.

For what types of marketing needs is Arcads AI pricing considered a powerful tool, despite its limitations?

Arcads AI pricing is particularly valuable for performance marketers, e-commerce brands, and those selling digital products who need to generate a high volume of UGC-style talking-head videos for A/B testing hooks and faces without the high cost of traditional production.
