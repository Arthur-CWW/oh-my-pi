Below you can find the information what content is not allowed on Arcads: These guidelines apply universally across all Arcads' plans, including Pro plans, and extend to all actor types, ensuring no exceptions are made in content enforcement.

⚠️ **Important note:** The Customer and users undertake not to use the video for any purpose that is prejudicial to the actors and in particular to their dignity, integrity and moral rights.

# 1. Adult Content

## a. No sexual services permitted

- Including: promotion or sale of adult entertainment venues like host/hostess clubs and strip clubs.
- Solicitation of escort services, prostitution, or erotic massages.
- Sexual chats, images, or pornography.
- Member-exclusive streaming of adult content via webcam (e.g., stripping, nude modeling, or masturbation).

## b. No sexual products permitted

- Including: promotion or sale of pornographic material, sex toys, and supplies like lubricants or fetish costumes.
- Promotion or sale of products enhancing sexual performance, pleasure, or bodily appearance, such as drugs, medications, or devices like penis enlargement or breast enhancement products.

## c. No sexual activity allowed

- Including: depictions of any sexual acts like penetrative sex, non-penetrative sex, and oral sex.
- Physical sexual arousal, including stimulation and responses to arousal.
- Fetish and kink activities.

## **d. Sexually explicit content, text, and audio**

- Depictions of specific sexual acts like grinding, humping, or imitating sexual positions.
- Text or audio directly describing sexual activity, body parts, or products.

## **e. Sexually suggestive content**

- Such as: overtly sexual kissing, suggestive text or audio, or flirtatious movements.
- Emphasis on sensitive body parts or suggestive body positions.

## **f. Nudity and body exposure**

- Including: displaying nudity or excessive skin, or portraying sensitive body parts.

## **g. Dating and live chat**

- Including: promotion of dating apps or services encouraging transactional relationships or sexual encounters, including infidelity or international brides.
- Engaging in relationships for compensation or material benefit, or promoting such arrangements

# 2. Illegal or Harmful Activities

- Constituting or encouraging criminal offenses or dangerous activities resulting in serious harm or death.
- Disseminating harmful false information inciting hatred, prejudice, or influencing civic processes.
- Threats, intimidation, or harassment, including content intended to mock, humiliate, or embarrass others.

# 3. Hateful or Offensive Content

- Content promoting violence or discrimination based on various factors like race, ethnicity, religion, gender, etc.

# 4. Content Harmful to Minors

- Content causing physical, mental, or moral harm to minors.

# 5. Promotion of Prohibited Activity

- Unlawfully circumventing technical or other access restrictions to infringe upon intellectual or other property rights
- Identity theft or the stealing of any personal information, including through the use of malware, spyware, or any unauthorised or unlawful means of obtaining information
- Document falsification, forgery, or counterfeiting
- Making deceptive claims, omitting information on fees, charges, and tariffs, or proposing harmful business models
- Unwarranted claims or misinformation, including pricing, discount, or promotion information inconsistency
- Missing terms and conditions or privacy policy pages

# 6. Violation of Advertising Rules

- Misleading or deceptive advertising.
- Cloning or modifying the image or voice of creators (e.g., deepfake).
- Using the video as input data for software altering image, voice, or speech.

# **7. Political Content**

- No promotion of political campaigns or candidates

Including: endorsement, promotion, or advertisement of political candidates, parties, or campaign materials.

- No discussion or debate on political topics

Including: public discussions, debates, or opinions on political subjects, ideologies, or policies.

- No election-related activities

Including: content promoting or discussing election processes, results, or voter information.

- No politically charged imagery or symbolism

Including: use of political symbols, logos, or imagery related to political movements or events.

- No political fundraising or solicitation

Including: fundraising efforts or donation solicitations for political campaigns or causes.

- No false information or propaganda

Including: dissemination of false information intended to influence political opinions or election outcomes.
