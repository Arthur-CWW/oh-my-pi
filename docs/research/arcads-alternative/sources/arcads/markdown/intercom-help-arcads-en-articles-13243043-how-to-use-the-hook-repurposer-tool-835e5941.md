Instead of guessing what might work, you can leverage **winning ad hooks** and adapt them to your product—maximizing your chances of creating high-performing, scroll-stopping ads.

## **What Is Hook Repurposer?**

**Hook Repurposer** is a tool within **Arcads** that analyzes a viral ad and recreates its opening hook using your own product context.

The tool focuses on **what actually drives attention**—pacing, structure, and messaging—while adapting the content to your brand.

## **👇 How to Use Hook Repurposer**

1. In the Composer, click **See more**
2. Select **Hook Generator**
3. Upload:

   - a viral ad video (used as a reference)
   - a picture of your product *(optional)*
   - a short prompt describing your product or brand
4. Generate — your **12-second hook** is ready to use

## **Important Note**

The feature only analyzes the **first 12 seconds** of the reference video.

Make sure your input video contains a **strong hook within that timeframe** for best results.

## **Key Benefits**

- Reuse proven viral hooks with your own product
- Increase your chances of creating high-performing ads
- Generate short, impactful hooks optimized for paid social
- Perfect for TikTok, Meta, and YouTube Shorts

## **Key Takeaway**

Great ads don’t start randomly—they start with strong hooks.

Hook Repurposer lets you replicate what already works and apply it to your brand in seconds.
