## **Overview**

**Skin Enhancer** improves the realism of fully AI-generated faces by refining skin texture and details, making them ready for use in high-quality videos.

By uploading an image of a person generated with AI, Skin Enhancer enhances facial realism so the result looks more natural, lifelike, and suitable for production use.

## **What Is Skin Enhancer?**

**Skin Enhancer** is a tool within **[Arcads](chatgpt://generic-entity?number=0)** designed to upgrade the visual quality of AI-generated faces.

It refines key skin elements such as:

- texture and natural variation
- lighting consistency
- tone balance
- fine facial details

The output is a more realistic, human-looking face that can be seamlessly used across any Arcads video workflow.

---

## **How It Works**

1. Upload an image of a fully AI-generated person
2. Apply **Skin Enhancer**
3. Arcads processes and enhances skin realism
4. Use the improved image directly in your video creation

## **Key Benefits**

- Significantly improves realism of AI-generated faces
- Reduces “plastic” or overly smooth skin appearance
- Makes avatars ready for close-up and talking-head videos
- Works across all video formats and models

---

## **Best Practices**

- Use high-resolution AI-generated images
- Ensure the face is clearly visible and well-lit
- Avoid extreme angles or heavy occlusion
- Apply Skin Enhancer before generating videos for best results
