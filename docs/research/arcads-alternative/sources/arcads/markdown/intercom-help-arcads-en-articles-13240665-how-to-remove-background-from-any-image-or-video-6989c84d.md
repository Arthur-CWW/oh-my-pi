Remove the background from **any photo or video in one click**.

Perfect for **clean product cutouts, UGC edits, green-screen style videos**, and fast creative workflows—no manual masking required.

## **Why use Background Removal**

- Create **studio-clean visuals** from raw content
- Repurpose UGC for ads, landing pages, and social
- Speed up creative production without editing tools

## **Features**

- **Upload an image or video** and instantly extract the subject
- **High-precision masking** with crisp edges and natural details
- Works with **people, products, and complex scenes**
- Preserves hair, hands, edges, and motion in video

## **👇 How to use it**

1. In the **Composer**, click **“See more”**
2. Select **“Remove Background”**
3. Upload your **image or video**
4. Generate your **clean cutout** in one click

That’s it—your subject is ready to use on any background, creative, or format.
