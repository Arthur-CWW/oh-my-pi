Creating high-quality product content shouldn't take days. With Arcads, it takes minutes.

This article walks you through the basics - what you'll see in the demo video and what you can do from day one. No technical skills needed, no studio required. Ready? 👇
​

# **📦 Step 1 - Upload Your Product**

Start by importing a product image into Arcads. Any image works, you can always improve it after.

- Click New Project and drag your image into the platform
- Arcads detects the product automatically, no manual cropping needed
- Your product is now ready to be placed in any scene or style

💡 *You can generate up to 100 free images per day, so don't hesitate to experiment.*

# **🖼️ Step 2 - Generate Images**

Once your product is uploaded, use a prompt (a simple text description) to generate new images in any context or aesthetic.

- Describe the scene you want, e.g. "a coffee shop in Paris" or "minimalist white studio"
- Choose the AI model that best fits your visual style
- Select your favorites and customize further: change colors, backgrounds, or elements on the character

💡 *Not sure how to write a prompt? Use the built-in Prompt Builder — click "See More" and search for it.*

# **🎬 Step 3 - Turn Images into Talking Videos**

Bring your visuals to life with Arcads' Talking Actor feature. Pick a character from your generated image and have them deliver your message on camera.

- Click "Talking Actor" and choose a voice from the library — or clone your own
- Add your script, Arcads can automatically add emotions to vary the tone
- Preview the audio before generating the full video
- Generate videos without any time limit long using the same workflow

💡 *Perfect for ads, product explainers, social content, and landing page videos.*

# **🎥 Step 4 - Add Movement or Create B-Rolls**

Want more than a static talking head? Arcads lets you add specific movements to your actor, or generate short B-roll clips to enrich your videos.

## **Animated Actors**

- Choose a movement model: Clean, Dance, Work, and more
- Set the duration and aspect ratio
- Generate, then swap the voice or add captions if needed

## **B-Roll Clips**

- Start from your product image and describe the shot you want
- Select a model like Seedance 2.0 and set a short duration (5–6 seconds)
- Generate multiple clips at once to pick the best ones

💡 *B-rolls are great for close-ups, product detail shots, or adding visual variety to your main video.*

# **🌍 Step 5 - Translate Your Content**

Reach a global audience without re-shooting anything. Arcads translates your videos automatically, matching lip movements to the new language.

- Click "See More" then "Translate"
- Choose your target language and accent
- Hit generate — your localized video is ready

# **⚡ Going Further - Automate with Workflows**

Once you're comfortable with the basics, Workflows let you automate the entire content creation process at scale.

Example: transform one winning ad into 20 UGC variations — with different actors, scripts, and languages — all in a single automated workflow.

- Click "New Workflow" and describe what you need
- Build nodes manually or let Arcads suggest a structure
- Run the workflow and get a full content batch, ready to publish

# **✅ What You Can Do with Arcads**

- Generate lifestyle images from any product photo
- Create talking actor videos with custom voices and scripts
- Add movement, B-rolls, and cinematic effects
- Translate your content into any language automatically
- Automate the whole process for large-scale content production

This demo covers the essentials - but there's a lot more to explore. Try it out and see what you can create.
