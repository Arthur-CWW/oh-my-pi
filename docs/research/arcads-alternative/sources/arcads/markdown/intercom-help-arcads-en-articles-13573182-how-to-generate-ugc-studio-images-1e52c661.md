## What is UGC Studio?

**UGC Studio** lets you generate highly aesthetic, organic-looking UGC creators using AI.
The model is trained on **thousands of aesthetic UGC examples**, allowing it to produce creators that feel natural, authentic, and ready for video.

---

## How It Works

1. Go to **<http://arcads.ai>** and click **Create image**.
2. Select **UGC Studio** as the model.
3. Generate your **UGC actor**.

4. Click **Turn into video**.
5. Create engaging videos with your UGC creator using **Veo 3.1**.

That’s it — from image to video in just a few clicks.
