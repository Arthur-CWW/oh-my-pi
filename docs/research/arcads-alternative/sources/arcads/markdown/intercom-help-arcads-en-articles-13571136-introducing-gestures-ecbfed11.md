## Detailed Description

**Gestures** lets you generate expressive motion clips for any charcter by **selecting one of our preset templates**.
​

Arcads will create a realistic reaction video that captures the intended emotion or movement.
​

This feature is perfect for **hooks, B-rolls, or any moment** where visual expression elevates storytelling and engagement.

---
