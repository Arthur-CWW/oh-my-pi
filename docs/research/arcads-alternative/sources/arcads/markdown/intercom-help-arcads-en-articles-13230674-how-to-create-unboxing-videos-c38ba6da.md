## **Overview**

**AI Unboxing** allows you to generate realistic unboxing videos that showcase your product from a **first-person** or **creator-style POV**, without filming or working with influencers.

This feature is designed for **UGC-style advertising**, helping brands create authentic-looking product demos at scale.

## **What Is AI Unboxing?**

**AI Unboxing** is a feature within Arcads that transforms a single product image into a high-quality unboxing video.

Simply upload an image of your product, select a background environment, and Arcads generates realistic unboxing footage in multiple styles, such as:

- hands-on unboxing
- close-up product reveals
- creator POV shots

It’s one of the fastest ways to produce **scroll-stopping product demos** that feel natural and authentic—without cameras, studios, or logistics.

## **Key Benefits**

- Generate **hundreds of unboxing videos** in minutes
- Showcase products in **realistic, creator-style POV** formats
- Optimized for **TikTok, Meta, and YouTube UGC ads**
- Eliminate costs related to product shoots, creators, and logistics

## **Limitations & Best Practices**

To get the best results, keep the following in mind:

- Works best with **clear, high-resolution product images**
- Transparent packaging or strong reflections may reduce realism
- Backgrounds are limited to the current preset library
- Text-heavy packaging may not render accurately
- Some products may have **dimension inconsistencies**
- Minor visual warping may occur in complex scenes
