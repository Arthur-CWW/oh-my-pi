## 1️⃣ Talking Actors: Longer, Structured Videos

The **Talking Actors** model is perfect for videos up to **2 minutes**. It works well for **educational content, product demos, and narrative-driven videos** that need a solid structure.

**How to use it:**

- Go to the template library and select one of our Arcads actors, then click **Add actors.**

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903666702/66f2eed20337848ac8dab62cfe83/f853e75f-599c-4532-8309-1df19e2ad9f7?expires=1780497900&signature=66a8cdcc1bdc1d6aeae22a68c43efe2e4d8571f5ebc99e949d47f8db7f985c7d&req=dSknFc94m4ZfW%2FMW1HO4zWrRAcGUadfIyogvvDM3ff%2BLYEpaB5ArpISdLOln%0ADNpz%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903666702/66f2eed20337848ac8dab62cfe83/f853e75f-599c-4532-8309-1df19e2ad9f7?expires=1780497900&signature=66a8cdcc1bdc1d6aeae22a68c43efe2e4d8571f5ebc99e949d47f8db7f985c7d&req=dSknFc94m4ZfW%2FMW1HO4zWrRAcGUadfIyogvvDM3ff%2BLYEpaB5ArpISdLOln%0ADNpz%0A)

  Filter them by **age, gender, type, and situation** to easily find the best match for your needs.

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667051/63e7c164b9c0bbf8ce1b107fe340/bf2f2a30-b8aa-4f66-8576-bbfd63d224fb?expires=1780497900&signature=1f77fdad76c3da94319d13e47e4e97acf1b869a9f73278f110ec5306c47f3548&req=dSknFc94moFaWPMW1HO4zSd2afNAjjdb7eeN0L5v7FTs40k2wRP%2BPO%2FCpjdr%0AaoAN%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667051/63e7c164b9c0bbf8ce1b107fe340/bf2f2a30-b8aa-4f66-8576-bbfd63d224fb?expires=1780497900&signature=1f77fdad76c3da94319d13e47e4e97acf1b869a9f73278f110ec5306c47f3548&req=dSknFc94moFaWPMW1HO4zSd2afNAjjdb7eeN0L5v7FTs40k2wRP%2BPO%2FCpjdr%0AaoAN%0A)
- Write your full script (what the actor should say) in the text field. **Tip:** Break it into sections for a smoother flow. You can also add **emotions and intonations** to make it more engaging (click add emotions)
  ​

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667486/643eed66b725eae4bd26988033c6/2cb60c30-5176-4dca-af22-c10dc0e47c82?expires=1780497900&signature=74f12fbba9a72f589e4d17081f65a437f282bbb87bf63b8ccec739de5fabe076&req=dSknFc94moVXX%2FMW1HO4zb9s6pH%2ForabRl%2FeE90cHyMAOAVVkOacCwRDLHoZ%0A1Kk3%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667486/643eed66b725eae4bd26988033c6/2cb60c30-5176-4dca-af22-c10dc0e47c82?expires=1780497900&signature=74f12fbba9a72f589e4d17081f65a437f282bbb87bf63b8ccec739de5fabe076&req=dSknFc94moVXX%2FMW1HO4zb9s6pH%2ForabRl%2FeE90cHyMAOAVVkOacCwRDLHoZ%0A1Kk3%0A)
- Choose a voice: filter by languages, accent, or create a custom one (Pro plan), or import a custom one from Eleven Labs (Pro plan).
  ​

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667712/0238497fba4202967761a23e0572/d8256069-1497-4a9a-a2f8-e1592f5bf01d?expires=1780497900&signature=19b26a2b190d5edda081e53fb563159075713f83a5f703d8d26109c65368a856&req=dSknFc94moZeW%2FMW1HO4za5bJww2li1eWkhP2K3eT3CqjzVdw3ZjNLfHQbws%0AW9cR%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903667712/0238497fba4202967761a23e0572/d8256069-1497-4a9a-a2f8-e1592f5bf01d?expires=1780497900&signature=19b26a2b190d5edda081e53fb563159075713f83a5f703d8d26109c65368a856&req=dSknFc94moZeW%2FMW1HO4za5bJww2li1eWkhP2K3eT3CqjzVdw3ZjNLfHQbws%0AW9cR%0A)

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903765533/0295baf9b7a695dd0c4f89889840/Capture+d%E2%80%99e%CC%81cran+2025-12-26+a%CC%80+17_09_33.png?expires=1780497900&signature=4f7254563e01566ac4344e884b4e8f4452feccdb98b31e876d455daa44d7156c&req=dSknFc54mIRcWvMW1HO4zVqfiurcyxpLuiBlY%2FQcq%2F3n%2BjuKBUNGykp9BodW%0APOP4%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903765533/0295baf9b7a695dd0c4f89889840/Capture+d%E2%80%99e%CC%81cran+2025-12-26+a%CC%80+17_09_33.png?expires=1780497900&signature=4f7254563e01566ac4344e884b4e8f4452feccdb98b31e876d455daa44d7156c&req=dSknFc54mIRcWvMW1HO4zVqfiurcyxpLuiBlY%2FQcq%2F3n%2BjuKBUNGykp9BodW%0APOP4%0A)
- Adjust voice settings:

  - **Pacing, stability, similarity :** to match your desired runtime
- Preview the audio before generating to ensure it sounds perfect.
- Click **Generate** — Arcads syncs the voice, animates the actor, and stitches everything together.
  ​

  [![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903669834/fd50365cb7366b4fcfb909fc38f9/e5d29616-37d0-452c-8d80-83e34c291a04?expires=1780497900&signature=105637f01c4f96db9ec185e896714f6f1ffa07450f684167d490ae7fb02c52f4&req=dSknFc94lIlcXfMW1HO4zZaCQ6bP%2F8aCnOVtMcgoEZTZ%2BZJGxbCwMfy%2FtfKI%0AASd5%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4j1iryr/1903669834/fd50365cb7366b4fcfb909fc38f9/e5d29616-37d0-452c-8d80-83e34c291a04?expires=1780497900&signature=105637f01c4f96db9ec185e896714f6f1ffa07450f684167d490ae7fb02c52f4&req=dSknFc94lIlcXfMW1HO4zZaCQ6bP%2F8aCnOVtMcgoEZTZ%2BZJGxbCwMfy%2FtfKI%0AASd5%0A)

This method is ideal for videos that are **narrative-driven and polished**, lasting up to two minutes.

## 2️⃣ Sora 2 actors available: Shorter, Ultra-Realistic Videos

The **Sora 2** method lets you create **shorter videos** with **super realistic actors**. It’s perfect for **quick, dynamic UGC-style clips** where visual credibility matters more than length.

**How to use it:**

- Open Arcads and select one of the available Sora 2 actors.

  e.g actor says with a british accent "your script"
