## **Overview**

Showing your app directly in a video is one of the most effective ways to:

- explain how your product works
- build trust with users
- increase conversion and adoption

With **Arcads**, there are multiple ways to showcase your app depending on the format and level of realism you need.

This article walks through the **best methods to display your app inside a video**, along with tips to keep it clean and engaging.

## **Create a 4-Second Video of an Actor Showing Your App (No Talking)**

## **Step 1: Prepare Your App Screenshot**

Download one of your app screenshots, aiming for the **highest quality possible**.

A clear, high-resolution image ensures the best visual result in the generated video.

**Tip:** Choose a screen that highlights a key feature or core value of your product.

### **Step 2 : Generate a video start frame**

Upload your app screen as a reference image in the Image Generation tool on Arcads.

Describe the image, the actor’s appearance, the situation, how they are holding and presenting the phone to the camera, and what they are saying.

exemple :

A large studio microphone on a stand points toward the woman. An open spiral-bound notebook with several scribbled markings is on a wooden surface.
