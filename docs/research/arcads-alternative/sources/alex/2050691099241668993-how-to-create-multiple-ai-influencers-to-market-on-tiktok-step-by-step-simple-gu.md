## The biggest thing most people get wrong

The internet already tells you what works.

Every viral TikTok, Reel, or Short is:

- proof of demand
- proof of structure
- proof of messaging
Your job is not to be creative from scratch.

Your job is to distribute intelligently.

## Step 1: Don’t guess. Learn from what already works

I scroll:

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
I look for:

- hooks in first 2–3 seconds
- strong retention
- real comments
Free market research at scale.

## Step 2: Generate unique AI influencers

Most people reuse one face.

I don’t.

Each influencer has:

- unique face
- unique vibe
- specific persona
Using NanoBanana:

- generate fast
- control style
- avoid repetition
Unique faces = scalability.

## Step 3: Realism beats perfection

- model aesthetic

- imperfect shots
- casual moments
- real-life environments
