# GPT Pro Prompt 05 — Product strategy, feature priority, and moat for Arcads alternative

You are my product strategist for a clean-room Arcads.ai alternative.

Use project sources and web browsing. I need a brutally practical answer: what parts of Arcads matter, what to replicate first, what to skip, and where a modular graph/layer product can beat them.

## Context

The product I want is not just another black-box AI ad generator. It should be a modular, editable creative instrument:

- asset catalog with provenance;
- format/template decomposition;
- reusable personas/actors/products/B-roll/caption styles;
- graph/layer recipes;
- deterministic post-production text/captions;
- batch variants and remix knobs;
- analytics feedback loop.

Clean-room constraints:

- copy workflow/format mechanics, not code/private identities/protected media/trademarks;
- user-provided/licensed media only;
- no platform-evasion tooling;
- safe disclosure/consent around AI actors and clones.

## Deliverables

### 1. What Arcads is really selling

Separate:

- commodity features;
- workflow convenience;
- real moat;
- marketing positioning;
- buyer psychology.

### 2. Feature prioritization

Make a P0/P1/P2/P3 table:

Feature | Why it matters | User pain | Evidence | Prototype complexity | Moat potential | Should we build now?

Include:

- actor/persona library;
- custom actor/avatar;
- product-to-UGC ad;
- script/hook generator;
- format/template library;
- reference-video/format decomposition;
- captions/subtitles;
- voice/TTS/lipsync;
- product-in-hand/show-app/unboxing;
- B-roll generator;
- batch variants;
- workflow canvas;
- editor-lite/timeline;
- API;
- competitor ad repurposer/spy agent;
- analytics/creative testing;
- publishing/scheduling integration.

### 3. Wedge strategy

What should a solo/fast prototype focus on to feel impressive fastest?

Give 3 wedge options:

- AI UGC ads for apps/products;
- AI influencer/persona farm for organic shortform;
- format-decomposition/slideshow/brainrot engine.

For each:

- target user;
- demo flow;
- moat;
- risks;
- fastest path;
- why it could beat Arcads or avoid competing head-on.

### 4. UX/workflow design

Design the UX for the first internal tool:

- inputs;
- template selection;
- persona selection;
- product asset intake;
- model selection;
- graph/layer view;
- batch generation;
- review/edit;
- export;
- metadata.

### 5. Product boundaries and platform constraints

Cover boundaries for:

- actor cloning;
- influencer/persona generation;
- reference video decomposition;
- protected media;
- ad claims;
- platform posting/scheduling;
- disclosure.

### 6. Next 30 days

Give a ruthless 30-day plan:

- weekly milestones;
- must-have demos;
- what to intentionally not build;
- success criteria.

Be opinionated. I want a plan I can actually execute.
