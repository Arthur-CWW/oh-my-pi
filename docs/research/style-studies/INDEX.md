# Style Studies

Cross-stream index for visual, motion, interaction, and generative-interface research used by Primer, Companion, Playground, and future creation-engine work.

## Contract

Style Studies keeps five layers distinct:

1. **Source archive** — faithful, provenance-rich public posts, images, videos, transcripts, and scene notes.
2. **Style study** — evidence-backed analysis of recurring choices, lineages, tensions, and campaign-specific variation.
3. **Primitive** — a reusable visual/interaction/motion rule with inputs, parameters, failure modes, and accessibility behavior.
4. **Prototype** — an executable experiment combining primitives around one product question.
5. **Adoption** — a stream-specific implementation with its own acceptance criteria and proof.

A source is inspiration, not a template. Do not copy motifs without understanding function, authorship, context, and accessibility. Do not call inferred lineage fact. Every study identifies which observations are direct, inferred, unresolved, or Arthur-labeled.

## Provisional vocabulary to test

Arthur’s current language is evidence for inquiry, not a settled taxonomy:

- EVA / NERV control-room graphics
- procedural systems aesthetics
- computational constructivism
- terminal or code-engine brutalism
- scientific-instrument minimalism
- generative diagrammatic motion
- speculative R&D propaganda
- esoteric research-institution branding
- technical editorialism
- machine-room typography

Poet Engineer and Nous Research may combine different subsets. Future studies should name observable components—type systems, composition, motion, diagram grammar, color, texture, sound, interaction, information density—before proposing historical or cultural lineage.

## Cross-stream use

- **Primer:** reading, marginalia, semantic navigation, source scraps, graph/creation-engine prototypes.
- **Companion:** ambient state, artifact feeds, spatial control surfaces, human-in-the-loop decisions.
- **Playground:** executable style studies, generative scenes, motion/diagram prototypes, model design portfolios.

Streams may consume this index and its trackable manifests. Large raw media stays in source-local ignored directories; do not duplicate binaries across streams.
