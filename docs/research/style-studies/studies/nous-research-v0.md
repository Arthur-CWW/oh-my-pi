## Campaign boundaries first

The visible window is not one coherent “Nous style.” It contains at least three authorship/context strata:

1. **Hermes product campaigns:** releases and capabilities (`Reach`, `Judgement`, `/learn`, blank slate, computer use, web speed, vaults, cloud/desktop).
2. **Nous institutional/research identity:** abstract/editorial marks, benchmark/model discourse, research-lab or dossier posture.
3. **External/collaborator campaigns:** NVIDIA × Stripe hackathon, StepFun, Tencent Hunyuan, CUA, Bitwarden/1Password, linked Hermes masterclass. These often inherit partner marks or independent production systems and must not be treated as universal Nous identity.

## Observable clusters

### NR-C1 — Hermes release dossiers

**Evidence:** [Reach release](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2068056222457115126), [Judgement release](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2072413332665962617).

- **Observed composition:** square, near-black frames; large technical headings; small explanatory copy; thin corner brackets/rules; small data/waveform marks. Sampled sequences retain a stable interface frame while headings and content modules change.
- **Typography:** display face resembles pixel/techno or squared grotesk; supporting text is compact sans/mono-like. This is a role distinction, not a recommendation to clone the face.
- **Color/texture:** white and gray on near-black with selective cyan/electric-blue brand payoff; distressed monochrome collage and noise occur in the Judgement sequence.
- **Temporal grammar:** topic card → capability/evidence modules → product/brand lockup; Judgement adds a more cinematic escalation (cosmic/radial image → distressed collage → fragmented grid → clean blue mark → blackout).
- **Production clue:** template motion graphics plus composited image treatment; stable frame makes successive content readable.
- **Failure mode:** importing “classified HUD” ornament without meaningful state or provenance.

### NR-C2 — capability instrument cards

**Evidence:** [/learn](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2069526242236182697), [MoA virtual models](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2070610321278988385), [web performance](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2071974594961977727), [cloud setup](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2074878754485043333), [desktop/cloud discovery](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2075675120442486931).

- **Observed motifs:** command-like slash headings, “RESTRICTED”/availability language, forms and rankings, diagrams/metrics, product UI glimpses, terminal or desktop captures.
- **Composition:** one claim per card, high heading-to-body scale contrast, persistent framing, then a screen or diagram proving function.
- **Interaction/UI behavior shown:** setup choices, agent state, search/ranking, terminal/GUI coexistence. These are product demonstrations inside promotional motion, not evidence that the product UI universally uses the campaign HUD.
- **Temporal grammar:** readable information modules and interface reveal; mostly semantic substitution in a fixed shell rather than continuous camera spectacle.
- **Inferred effect:** operational confidence and restricted-access drama. The latter should be avoided where openness and inspectability are the actual product values.

### NR-C3 — research/editorial diagram mode

- **Observed:** sampled frames include white-background charts, sparse line/bar graphics, wireframe cube, and text-heavy explanatory slides; the poster overview includes a blue-on-white editorial family with ruled cards and a large serif “FEATURES” treatment.
- **Typography:** serif display can coexist with mono/sans labels; hierarchy comes from contrast between editorial headline and technical annotation.
- **Color:** white/cream field with cobalt/ultramarine lines and text, sharply distinct from the black Hermes launch system.
- **Temporal grammar:** diagram sequence → explanatory text → end card/black. Comprehension-oriented pacing is likely, but exact dwell time requires shot timing analysis.
- **Suitable use:** method explanation, benchmark caveats, experiment manifests. Not a generic decoration layer.

### NR-C4 — machinic specimen / surveillance montage

- **Observed:** poster frames show high-contrast grayscale photography, CRT/computing imagery, image windows, tiny data columns, specimen-like central objects, scan noise, dither/halftone, grain, and thin measurement rules.
- **Composition:** central specimen or image field, peripheral panels, ticks, small labels, asymmetric evidence windows.
- **Texture:** intentionally degraded or reproduced image surface contrasts with very crisp vector overlays.
- **Temporal grammar from representative samples:** persistent HUD plus changing central figure/object; micro-variation reads as scan, monitoring, or analysis rather than conventional slideshow.
- **Inferred lineage:** scientific imaging, broadcast/surveillance displays, xerographic editorial collage. “EVA/NERV” is an Arthur-supplied resemblance hypothesis, not demonstrated source lineage.
- **Anti-caricature:** scanlines and warning ticks without a measured object are costume.

### NR-C5 — abstract model/partner kinetics

**Evidence:** [StepFun offer](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2071780437228138838), [Tencent Hunyuan Hy3](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2074260103469892045).

- **Observed:** black field, electric cyan/blue ribbons, glossy orb or flowing streak, isolated partner and Nous marks.
- **Temporal grammar:** abstract form → partner identity → brand association, with smoother morph/wipe behavior than the dossier cards.
- **Campaign status:** collaborator/co-brand work. Do not generalize partner color or logo choreography to institutional identity.

### NR-C6 — mascot and personable agent state

**Evidence:** [animated pet feature](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2069577310055846267).

- **Observed:** named mascot/character cards (“Boba,” “Dwight” visible in sampled frames), many small avatar icons, Hermes title, then dark desktop/chat UI.
- **Narrative:** individual character → population/choice → functional agent surface.
- **Interaction:** the post explicitly says sprites respond to idle, tool-running, thinking, waiting, finishing, and failing states. This is strong evidence for stateful ambient representation.
- **Failure mode:** cute motion that conceals status, creates distraction, or becomes the sole non-color state indicator.

### NR-C7 — hackathon/event propaganda

**Evidence:** [office hours](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2069150335386456283), [hackathon deadline](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2071681697234420127).

- **Observed:** collaborator-heavy copy, compact event cards, dark dramatic imagery, and deadline rhetoric.
- **Boundary:** NVIDIA/Stripe/event art direction is external/collaborative. It may evidence energetic campaign variation, not durable research identity.

### NR-C8 — atmospheric and astronomical identity fragments

- **Observed:** monochrome cloudscape; a “Sol / Luna / Terra” astronomical card; stark tree-shadow photography; abstract blue light and black fields in the poster set.
- **Function:** pause, scale, mystery, or institutional mood rather than capability explanation.
- **Unresolved:** exact post association for standalone images is a documented archive gap; do not assign campaign authorship or dates.

## Cross-cutting grammar

| Layer | Observed devices | Responsible abstraction |
|---|---|---|
| Type | squared/pixel display, compact sans/mono labels, occasional editorial serif | named roles: signal, annotation, editorial, data—not one mandatory font |
| Composition | fixed instrument shell, central specimen, peripheral evidence, ruled editorial cards, isolated mark | layout recipes selected by information task |
| Color | near-black/gray/white; selective cyan/blue; distinct white/cobalt editorial mode | palette families with contrast targets and semantic accent limits |
| Texture | crisp vectors against grain, dither, distressed collage, photographic haze | optional evidence/media treatment; never degrade essential text |
| Motion | modular card substitution, scan/micro-variation, montage escalation, smooth co-brand morph, stateful sprite | named temporal recipes with reduced-motion equivalents |
| UI | terminal/desktop capture, ranks/forms, command headings, state avatars | show real state/data; distinguish demo framing from product chrome |

## Lineage discipline

Arthur’s “sci-fi minimalism,” “scientific-instrument minimalism,” “speculative R&D propaganda,” and “esoteric research-institution branding” describe perceived effects. Plausible formal lineages include scientific imaging, technical manuals, surveillance/broadcast graphics, constructivist information hierarchy, editorial collage, and terminal culture. These are **inferred**, not author-labeled. No captured artifact establishes intentional Evangelion/NERV influence.

## Cross-stream uses and anti-patterns

- **Primer:** evidence panels, provenance labels, research/editorial diagrams, source scraps; quiet instrument grammar around authored text.
- **Companion:** ambient task-state representation, telemetry strips, capability proof, human decision points.
- **Playground:** motion recipes, specimen viewers, parameterized dossier/editorial variants, collaborator-aware campaign generators.
- **Avoid:** one universal black/cyan theme; false “RESTRICTED” theater; illegible microtype; texture over source text; partner branding absorbed as house style; terminal syntax where no command model exists; mascots without accessible textual state; copying logos, characters, layouts, or campaign assets.
