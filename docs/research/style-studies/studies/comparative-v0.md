Both bodies resist a flat ornamental reading. Their most reusable commonality is **inspectable transformation**: something enters a system, a rule changes it, the viewer can see state or variation, and the output remains tied to process.

## Differences and productive tensions

1. **Instrument vs campaign.** Poet’s captured discourse centers creative instruments and generators; Nous’s archive contains polished promotional narratives. A creation engine should preserve the former’s open-ended parameter exploration without inheriting the latter’s launch-theater pressure.
2. **Continuous vs editorial time.** Poet describes gesture, memory decay, curves, and traces—continuous/accumulative behaviors. Nous frequently uses discrete information cards and montage. Prototypes can choose temporal grammar by question rather than blending both indiscriminately.
3. **Semantic openness vs declarative claim.** Poet visualizes possibility and emergence; Nous often asserts capability, ranking, availability, or release. Primer needs qualified evidence; Companion may sometimes need decisive operational state.
4. **Generative scene vs composited dossier.** Poet repeatedly shows data/procedural geometry reconfiguring inside continuous 3D or radial fields; Nous often stabilizes a graphic shell and substitutes information modules or cuts through a campaign montage. Both use black technical fields, but Poet’s recovered surfaces are generally crisper, more spatial, and more visibly generative; Nous more often adds distressed editorial texture and declarative hierarchy.
5. **Authored eccentricity vs institutional coherence.** Poet’s multilingual glyphs and poetic titles personalize a research process. Nous’s repeated shells create campaign recognition. A cross-stream system needs variation without becoming either arbitrary or corporate-template monotony.

## Anti-caricatures

- **Not “cyberpunk”:** black + cyan + scanline is too coarse. Ask what is being measured, why the accent exists, and what changes over time.
- **Not fake control room:** no gauges, ticks, coordinates, or alerts without data semantics.
- **Not graph wallpaper:** geometry must answer a question; stable focus, relation type, provenance, and list/matrix fallback are mandatory.
- **Not terminal cosplay:** use command syntax only for executable commands, real event streams, diffs, or source code.
- **Not illegibility as sophistication:** microtype is metadata, never essential prose; texture cannot degrade reading.
- **Not mysticism by random glyph:** multilingual symbols need source, meaning, or generative role.
- **Not institutional overclaim:** “restricted,” rankings, and benchmark authority require evidence and caveats.
- **Not motion wallpaper:** every transition must reveal state, order, construction, difference, or causality; reduced motion must retain the same fact.
- **Not source laundering:** collaborator art, character assets, logos, and exact compositions remain source-owned.
- **Not one Arthur aesthetic:** preserve labels by prototype, stream, task, and date.

## Stream translation

- **Primer:** prioritize authored sequence, evidence tiers, recurrence trails, source scraps, and semantic zoom. Atmosphere must stay subordinate to reading.
- **Companion:** use stateful ambient indicators, trace histories, decision gates, and telemetry only for real agent/process state.
- **Playground:** expose full parameter space, seeds, motion recipes, and side-by-side model variants; it is the proper home for higher visual risk.
