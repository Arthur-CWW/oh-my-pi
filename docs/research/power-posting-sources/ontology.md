# Theoryposting — the ontology behind "conceptual power poaster"

*Fable synthesis, 2026-07-08. Grounded in: Meltdown (full text, `meltdown.md`), teortaxes longposts (`teortaxes-longposts.md`), repligate timeline samples, Arthur's account list.*

## The genre

**Theory-fiction compressed to feed velocity.** Land/CCRU's founding move: writing where the concept doesn't describe a future but attempts to summon it — *hyperstition*, "fictions that make themselves real." The accounts on this list transposed that from 90s samizdat to the timeline. Genre name: **hyperstitional theoryposting**. Practitioners: **theoryposters**.

## Invariants (the vibe's load-bearing walls)

1. **Declarative metaphysics** — ontological claims as news bulletins, zero hedging. "It's Intelligence-Complete." "There is an interior and it is busy and your watching is incidental to it." Confidence IS the argument.
2. **Compression as authority** — the aphorism implies a vast unposted system. You're reading excerpts of a book that doesn't exist.
3. **Stakes maximalism** — every local observation routed through species/deep-time scale ("2023–Deep Time"; a benchmark → civilizational verdict).
4. **Load-bearing technical substance** — mysticism sitting ON mechanism (repligate's koans backed by KV-cache information-flow analysis). This separates it from vibes-posting; the awe is earned by plumbing. This is the "smart TikTok" ingredient.
5. **Persona as instrument** — the account is a character/egregore (j⧉nus glyphs; the DeepSeek-stan oracle; roon's shitpost armor). Voice does work argument can't.
6. **The post performs its concept** — Meltdown's prose accelerates and sheds syntax while describing acceleration. A theorypost is a live demo of its own idea executing in the reader's attention.

## Why it's an oral medium (Arthur's instinct, confirmed)

Read aloud, theoryposts become incantations — sermon cadence, prophetic register. Pleometric's format = machine-liturgy visuals + a voice declaiming a compressed idea. The power-posting video lane is **giving theoryposts their pulpit**: narration (MiniMax voice), concept-derived visuals (scene.v1 machine aesthetics), possibly a hyper-real character delivering it (lip-sync lane, later).

One-liner: *They don't explain ideas — they detonate them. The feed as the last serious philosophical journal, delivered as prophecy.*
