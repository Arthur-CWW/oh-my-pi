## 1. The format, precisely

### 1.1 Openers — two doors, both slam

Every voiced piece opens on one of exactly two moves. There is no third.

**Door A — the hook question** *that is the whole thesis compressed.* Not a teaser; the argument itself, phrased as a question so the body becomes the proof.

> **"How many GPUs does it take to get rid of you?"** — `2054594912717312476`
> **"Your boss is a dominatrix. What now?"** — `2049999955834568734`
> **"Which video is AI?"** — `2059338854566355273`

The GPU opener is the masterclass: it sounds like a joke ("how many X does it take to Y"), it names the stakes (your job), and the entire 89-second explainer exists to answer it literally. The last line *closes the loop on the opener* — "It's really about how many GPUs it takes to do your job." Ring composition. The question is the container.

**Door B — the cold declarative**, an ontological claim stated as a weather report, zero ramp-up:

> **"We're in the middle of the excruciatingly fast takeoff."** — `2050364782411202781`
> **"Now is the time to act. Stop thinking."** — `2068025479026593909`
> **"Now let me be clear."** — `2049905101309972624` (a 4-second whole video that is *only* the throat-clear — the format joke is that the clarity never arrives)

Rule: **the first sentence contains the entire stake.** No "hey guys," no "so I was thinking," no context ramp. You are already mid-avalanche.

### 1.2 Sentence rhythm — breath-length, monosyllabic, one clause per beat

The spoken pieces are written for the lungs, not the eye. Measured across the GPU explainer, sentences run **~8–16 words**, overwhelmingly subject-verb-object, overwhelmingly monosyllabic:

> "So what's an agent? We can model that as a technology that converts compute into cognitive output. An hour of GPU use goes in, an hour of analyst equivalent work comes out. Agents are tech and not workers. That distinction is the important part." — `2054594912717312476`

Note the machine there: **a self-posed question** ("So what's an agent?") to pivot, then a definition, then a concrete image ("an hour goes in, an hour comes out"), then the flat verdict ("Agents are tech and not workers"), then the meta-instruction telling you which sentence to remember ("That distinction is the important part"). This is a rhythm you can reuse verbatim: *pivot-question → definition → image → verdict → signpost.*

And the **enumerated triple** — the single most TTS-durable structure in the corpus:

> "The wage ceiling for these substitutable tasks comes out to three things multiplied together. One, how productive you are relative to an agent. Two, how much compute one hour of agent work burns. Three, the rental rate of that compute." — `2054594912717312476`

Then the number injection — always concrete, always cheap-sounding-until-it-isn't:

> "H100s rent for about $2 an hour. A frontier model burns roughly one GPU hour per hour of senior knowledge work. If you're as productive as the agent, your ceiling lands around $2 an hour." — `2054594912717312476`

### 1.3 Register shift — deadpan is the carrier wave; absurd and rigorous are the sidebands

The voice never changes *tone*. It changes *content* under a fixed flat delivery. The governing law:

> **The more absurd the premise, the more rigorously deadpan the treatment — and the more rigorous the premise, the more absurd the frame it's delivered in.** Deadpan is the invariant; absurdity and rigor trade places around it.

Two proofs, opposite directions:

- **Absurd premise, rigorous treatment.** "Your boss is a dominatrix" is played entirely straight as a corporate compliance module, complete with legal disclaimer cadence: *"JPMango is not responsible for drug smoothies, latex-related injuries, unauthorized access to your bank account, performed at private members' clubs."* — `2049999955834568734`. The horror is that the *form* never breaks.
- **Rigorous premise, absurd frame.** Real algebraic number theory delivered as a horror-movie voiceover: *"Then, the construction is frightening. Let A be the product of one prime from each conjugal area. Its norm is p the d."* — `2057289057483346095`. The math is real; "frightening" is the tell that we're in a genre.

The register *shift within a piece* is usually a **single hard pivot near the end** — the explainer earns its rigor for 70 seconds, then snaps to a wider frame: "Older theory said high skill cognitive workers win... But with AI agents, the split actually runs through cognitive work itself. The dividing line moved inside the white collar category." That widening — from mechanism to civilizational verdict — is the stakes-maximalism move (ontology invariant 3), and it always happens **after** the plumbing, never before.

### 1.4 Lengths

Two natural durations, and almost nothing in between:

- **The explainer** — 45–90s. Room for a mechanism: definition, triple, numbers, reframe. (GPU, Aschenbrenner, CM fields, dominatrix.)
- **The incantation / stinger** — 4–27s. One idea, one image, one button. (Remove your brain; I'm going to work and laugh; Which video is AI?; the AI-video fairytales.)

For our lane the sweet spot is the **incantation-plus**: 25–40s, ~70–110 spoken words — long enough to build one mechanism or one escalation, short enough to be a single held breath. All three scripts below target it.

### 1.5 Endings — three exits, never a fade

The ear cannot re-scan, so the last line does all the work. The corpus uses exactly three:

1. **Mic-drop reframe** — restate the opener at a higher altitude: *"So stop thinking about the labor market and start thinking about the compute market. It's really about how many GPUs it takes to do your job."* — `2054594912717312476`
2. **Cliff / open loop** — a refrain or a threat that keeps running after the cut: *"I hope you like speed because it's only gonna get faster."* — `2050364782411202781`; *"Let's see if he keeps being aware of the situation."* — `2056378137831682359`
3. **Absurd button** — a short deadpan non-sequitur that detonates the frame: *"Consent is a two-way pollination."* — `2049999955834568734`; *"you are expelled, and also you are a witch. The young woman did not know this about herself."* — `2067386489403503090`; *"Now let's dance! Yay! Let's dance!"* — `2064045757515063502`.

**Never** a summary ("so that's why..."), never a call to subscribe played straight (the CTA is itself absurdist: *"Like this post? Love Pleometric. Together, we can do it."* — `2063699116991869299`).

## 2. The vibe, named — the ontology corrected by the oral record

Fable's six invariants (ontology.md §Invariants) hold. The transcripts **confirm** them and force **three additions** that only appear once you listen instead of read.

**Confirmed, with oral evidence:**

- **Inv. 1 — Declarative metaphysics.** Confirmed and *intensified* in speech. "This is not normal for a house." (`2067386489403503090`) is an ontological bulletin delivered flatter than any post could manage. The voice's flatness *is* the zero-hedging.
- **Inv. 4 — Load-bearing technical substance.** The single hardest-to-fake ingredient, and the transcripts prove it is non-negotiable: the GPU piece is a real (if stylized) compute-market argument; the CM-field piece is real algebraic number theory; the Aschenbrenner piece reads an *actual* 13F position by position. The awe is earned by plumbing. **This is the "smart TikTok" ingredient — remove it and you have generic doomer content.**
- **Inv. 6 — The post performs its concept.** In video this becomes literal: the acceleration piece *should* accelerate its cuts; "I hope you like speed" plays over speeding visuals. The concept executes in the viewer's attention.

**Added invariants — spoken-native, weak or absent in the written corpus:**

- **Inv. 7 — The hook-question-as-thesis.** Written theoryposts open on *assertion* ("It's Intelligence-Complete"). Spoken pleometric adds a second front door: a **question that is the argument compressed**, which the body then answers literally and the ending re-closes. This is the oral innovation. Roughly half the voiced pieces use it; it barely exists in the posts.
- **Inv. 8 — Flatness is the authority.** The ontology says "confidence IS the argument." In the mouth this sharpens: **the deadpan monotone is the load-bearing tone.** Menace is quiet. Excitement would break the spell. The narration never raises its voice, never "sells" — even "Now is the time to act. Act. Keep moving." (`2068025479026593909`) is delivered like an airline safety card. Loudness is the one thing that reads as fake.
- **Inv. 9 — The register-collapse joke.** Wrapping apocalyptic or obscene content in a **customer-service / onboarding / finance-desk register** is the most video-native move in the corpus and is nearly absent from the written feed. JPMango's HR training module, the Pangram detector's children's-TV cheer (*"Now let's dance! Yay!"*), Aschenbrenner's finance-bro tic (*"aware of the situation"*, *"get"*) — the humor is the *form* being wrong for the *content*, held without a wink. This is a whole engine the posts don't have because a post can't *perform* a genre; a voice can.

One-liner, upgraded for the lane: *They don't explain ideas, they detonate them — in the flattest possible voice, wearing the uniform of whatever institution the idea is destroying.*

## 3. Written theorypost vs spoken pleometric — the divergence, concretely

This is the crux, because our feedstock (Land, teortaxes, apralky, repligate, xeno) is **written** and our lane is **spoken**. They do not transfer 1:1. Here is exactly how the adaptation works.

### 3.1 What survives TTS unchanged

- **Declarative confidence** — a flat claim reads even better aloud than on the page.
- **The enumerated list / ticker** — "One... Two... Three...", or Aschenbrenner's position-by-position roll call. The ear loves a list; it's the most durable structure.
- **Short refrains** — "Nothing human makes it out of the near-future." "It's war." "We are not a serious species!" These were *written* to be chanted and lose nothing.
- **The mic-drop reframe** — a single wide sentence at the end.
- **Concrete numbers** — "$2 an hour", "5000 people", "forty-seven million".

### 3.2 What breaks in TTS and must be rewritten or cut

- **Nested subordinate clauses.** The ear has no back-button. apralky's flagship is the definitive example — a genuinely excellent argument rendered unspeakable:

  > "...which is equal to how much you expect your piece to be worth in case of a successful expropriation of capital, e.g. AI labs, weighted by the probability of success, less what you're already making as a wage (and less the redistributed benefit + the cost of revolt)." — `apralky-posts.md` §1

  Four levels deep with two parentheticals. No human voice recovers from this. **The adaptation is amputation to the spine:** *"The UBI they pay you to not riot has to scale with what you'd gain by rioting."* One clause. Same idea. (Worked in full as the bonus script, §6.)
- **Parentheticals and citations.** Land's "[DG1:239-40]", xeno's "Ewww ... [links: RealClearPolitics]" — a spoken piece can't read a footnote. Cut, or convert to a spoken aside.
- **Neologism pile-ups without breath.** Land's "meltdown acceleration, cyberian invasion, schizotechnics, K-tactics, bottom-up bacterial welfare, efficient neo-nihilism, voodoo antihumanism..." (meltdown.md ¶74) is thrilling on the page and **mush in the ear** past item three. Keep three, drop the rest, or let the *visuals* carry the list while the voice takes one.
- **Glyph / orthographic puns.** j⧉nus's glyphs, CJK visual puns (万花筒) — these are *type treatments*, not narration. They move from the voice track to the type track.
- **Hyperlink-dependent lines.** Any post whose meaning lives in the quote-tweet it's attached to (xeno's "Ewww", teortaxes quote-dunks) must be made self-contained or dropped.

### 3.3 The adaptation workflow (three moves)

1. **SPLICE** — pull the 2–4 most oral sentences from the source; discard the scaffolding. You are curating, not summarizing.
2. **GLUE** — add at most one short connective sentence in the house voice to bridge the splice. Glue must be flat, monosyllabic, and invisible.
3. **LAND** — end on a refrain that already exists in the source ("Nothing human makes it out of the near-future"; "We are not a serious species"). Never invent the button; the source almost always contains it.

The reassuring finding: **pleometric already writes at breath-length.** The GPU piece needs zero adaptation — it *is* the target form. Our job with Land/teortaxes/apralky is to get their written density *down* to where pleometric already lives.

## 4. Register palette (6 registers, one exemplar each)

The lane is not one voice; it is one *delivery* (deadpan) across six *content registers*. Every script picks one primary register and may hard-cut to a second at the button.

1. **AGI-economics explainer** *(flagship — the crown jewel).* Rigorous mechanism, hook-question, triple, numbers, mic-drop. Feedstock: pleometric GPU piece; apralky's redistribution model (once amputated).
   > "But agents inherit their supply from compute. That means they need fabs and energy, as well as water, land, and the right passport for buying chips." — `2054594912717312476`

2. **Markets / financial satire.** Bloomberg-terminal cadence, ticker enumeration, the finance-guy verbal tic held as a refrain. The comedy is the deadpan competence.
   > "He's holding his Bloom Energy calls... Now let's see the cuts... He's taking profits on the Bitcoin miner to AI data center trade. These names ran, and now he's rotating, get. ... Let's see if he keeps being aware of the situation." — `2056378137831682359`

3. **Corporate / institutional absurdism.** *(Inv. 9 engine.)* The uniform of HR, onboarding, product-demo, or children's television, worn over content that should shatter it — and it never shatters.
   > "Hi, JPMangoins. I'm Carol from People and Culture. Today, we're going to talk about a tricky workplace situation that affects one in three of you." — `2049999955834568734`

4. **Math horror.** A real theorem read as a summoning. The rigor is genuine; the genre framing ("frightening", "the construction") is the joke and the dread at once.
   > "Suppose, optimistically, that k is a high-degree cm field in which a fixed rational prime p splits completely into principal prime ideals. Then, the construction is frightening." — `2057289057483346095`

5. **Accelerationist mantra / power-posting proper.** Imperative, hypnotic, shedding syntax as it goes (Inv. 6 performing itself). Short. This is the register the *whole lane is named after*.
   > "Stop thinking. You do not have the time to think. Act. Keep moving. Your thoughts are not your friends. Remove your brain. Polish it until it is smooth and shiny, and then keep going." — `2068025479026593909`
   > (loop form: "I'm going to work and laugh, I'm going to work and laugh, I'm going to work and laugh" — `2055420576714408309`)

6. **AI-video deadpan narration.** Flat, present-tense, fairytale-grammar voiceover riding uncanny generated footage — "AI slop" reframed as deadpan literature. Simple declaratives, dream-logic causality, an absurd verdict button.
   > "The young woman discovers she is allergic to her own house. Every time she touches the wall, sparks come out from her hand. This is not normal for a house." — `2067386489403503090`
   > "It's more than a biological android. Sometimes they break." — `2067662803721220310`

*(apralky's macro-economics absurdism lives across registers 1 and 5: the redistribution model is register-1 mechanism; "farming infinity wealth and status right now", "We are not a serious species!" are register-5 aphorism. He is the reason register 1 has a *written* long-form supplier and register 5 has fresh buttons.)*

## 5. Do / Never — the writing rules for new scripts

**DO**
- Open on a hook-question-as-thesis (Door A) or a cold ontological declarative (Door B). The first sentence carries the entire stake.
- Write at breath length: one clause per line, subject-verb-object, mostly monosyllables. Read it aloud on one breath per line or rewrite it.
- Keep the delivery deadpan and *quiet* no matter how extreme the content. Flatness is the authority.
- Earn the awe with one real mechanism — a number, a theorem, a market, a model. No plumbing, no lane.
- Use an enumerated triple and/or a concrete cheap-sounding number. Both survive TTS and both read as competence.
- Route the local claim to civilizational / deep-time scale — but only *after* the mechanism, as the widening move.
- Land on a mic-drop reframe, an open-loop refrain, or an absurd button. When adapting, steal the button from the source.
- Let the visuals perform the concept and carry any list the voice can't (acceleration → accelerating cuts; a neologism swarm → a scatter field).

**NEVER**
- Never hedge. No "might", "sort of", "I think" — *unless* it's the deadpan-uncertain button ("I don't know that it does", `2067431750951690447`).
- Never nest clauses, stack parentheticals, or read a citation/hyperlink aloud. The ear has no back-button.
- Never pile more than three neologisms in one breath (Land's TTS failure mode).
- Never explain the joke. The register collapse *is* the joke; a wink kills it.
- Never raise the voice or "sell". Excitement reads as fake; menace is quiet.
- Never end on a summary or a played-straight CTA. End on a detonation.
- Never let the AI palette creep in: no cyan-on-dark, no purple gradient, no glassmorphism. The lane's ink is **bone `#f2ead9` + vermilion `#ff3b1d` on warm near-black `#0a0810`** (established across `type-glitch`, `clone-field-pulse`). Printed, molten, machine — not nightclub.

### Script (c) — "The Number With No Name" *(ORIGINAL — the audition)*
