## Walls hit / provenance caveats

- **Meltdown opening was silently truncated by reader-mode extraction.** The default (trafilatura) fetch of ccru.net dropped the famous opening ("The story goes like this…") and two body paragraphs, starting mid-text at "Beyond the Judgement of God." Caught by cross-checking the canonical opening via web search, then re-fetched with `:raw` and reconstructed the full text from the HTML `<p>` structure. `meltdown.md` is the complete piece.
- **Encoding artifact in the CCRU source.** The iso-8859-1 page renders `&aelig;` ("æ") where an opening single-quote belongs (a known mojibake, confirmed against the *Fanged Noumena* / *A Nick Land Reader* editions). Normalized that single artifact to `'`; all genuine CCRU-era typos are preserved verbatim and listed in the file's fidelity note.
- **Nitter rate-limiting (HTTP 429).** `nitter.tiekoetter.com` served profile/status pages reliably but throttled the *search* endpoint repeatedly (and, late in the run, deep timeline paging too). Handled with sequential requests + pauses, falling back to profile-timeline paging and direct status-page reads. The one alternate mirror tried (xcancel.com) returned 403/failed, so all captures are from tiekoetter. No auth used; public surfaces only.
- **@teortaxesTex "long posts" are often short.** His highest-engagement posts are one-line zingers or screenshots-of-text (images, not archived as text per the no-media rule); his genuine long-form appears as self-threads and dense single paragraphs, which is what was selected.
- **@apralky quote-tweets.** Several of his best posts are quote-tweets where the mirror surfaced only the quoted account's stats; those engagement figures are marked "n/a via mirror". His essay-length work is on Substack (katechon99) and in X "Articles" — listed as leads, not reproduced (out of scope for verbatim tweet capture).
- **@pleometric is text-light + media-heavy.** Most of his timeline is short quips and "Pleometric Update" video posts; the substantive text lives in a handful of self-threads (process/technique + conceptual), which is what was selected. Video files are excluded per the no-media rule (archived separately) and video-bearing posts are flagged; deep paging for older process threads was cut short by rate-limiting.
- **@xenocosmography posts aphoristically.** He rarely writes long single tweets; his X long-form is threads and gematria breakdowns, and his essay-length work lives on his Substack (out of scope). The sample captures his best on-theme threads + representative theory hits, with a style note. His timeline also interleaves edgy hard-right political quote-tweets, which were deliberately excluded from the feedstock.
- **Engagement figures are mirror snapshots** (likes / views as of retrieval). For quote-tweets and thread-internal parts the mirror often surfaced only partial stats; those are marked "n/a via mirror" or given as approximate. Numbers are supplementary — every item carries the mandatory URL + date.
- **File deletion is blocked (EPERM)** in this workspace, so the obsolete `yungmacro-posts.md` could not be removed; it was overwritten with a pointer to `apralky-posts.md` instead.
