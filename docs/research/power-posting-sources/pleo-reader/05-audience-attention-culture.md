## ai-video-users
- **Date:** 2026-04-08
- **Status URLs (in order):** https://x.com/pleometric/status/2041887759783088615 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2041888287254565002
- **Source:** [pleometric-longposts.md §16](../pleometric-longposts.md#16-ai-video-users)

> I've been getting dms from young IG and TT users asking me how to make AI videos. "What app do you use?" I've talked to few of them and they either don't own a laptop, or they rarely use it. According to their profiles most of them are in the US, so it's not strictly an access issue. This is a very small sample size, like 12 conversations or so but it does add to my growing suspicion that younger cohorts are using short form as a stand in for apps.
> They "prompt" accounts that post AI content to tune the videos closer to what they want in lieu of having model access. They at best use GPT image gen (and previously SORA), the more savvy ones game free tiers from companies like heygen and etc to make their own variations, which they often find lacking.

> "what app do you use?" is most often followed by "is it free?" so you're not really looking at potential customers here. There are hundreds of model wrappers out there, these users don't want to or can't pay, but they do want to play with AI video and image.

## positive-ai-messaging
- **Date:** 2026-04-09
- **Status URLs (in order):** https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042255156637499561 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042255361915166763 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042255747417854434 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042257713619501519 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042261516238340345 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042263582402850876 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042265631328796899 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042269692086100161 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042284607408648355 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2042286702551576821
- **Source:** [pleometric-longposts.md §17](../pleometric-longposts.md#17-positive-ai-messaging)

> There's almost no _broad_ positive coverage of AI, so the space is dominated by negative press. Negative framing from pr cycles diffuses outward from insiders to communicators to the public. What most people hear is a mix of disinfo and doom. Fun doomposting becomes media fodder

> "AI will cure cancer" and "double human lifespans" are abstractions compared to the stomach punch of "your job is gone in 12 months".
> You really want to double click on the concrete, tangible advances part. Supply chains, coordinating issues and etc.

> I should try broad access positive content production. Youtube is a good starting place.

> Bryan Johnson understands the trojan horse of levity. His erection talk is the container for his brand's core messaging of why one should care about longevity. It captures attention and lowers defenses. This is skillful comms in social media, you want that but for AI.

> The positive case for AI is personal and experiential. What is the individual getting out it? There sure seems to be a lot of token demand and fascination by Openclaw type narratives. Low friction positives beat the broad narrative claims, the erection is a proxy for health.

> AI needs to be fruit love island with openclaw characteristics. "But that's slop!" yes, and what does that tell you about messaging?

> We need fewer would-be AI priests and more proselytizers. The gospel of Meltdown loses against Fruit Love Island evangelism

> dozens of these high viewership accounts identified demand and are optimizing on top of it.
> Hundreds of such videos aimed at broad audiences.
> Keep in mind that for most people OAI = AI, generalize from this to understand the sentiment landscape.

> this is by searching "open ai"

> the issue is: you'd have to say AI slop is good, actually. No one is brave enough
