## platform-formats

> Platforms have decided they basically like two formats: streaming and short form. Both have better retention metrics and drive sales better than long form.

> long form will probably go the way of documentaries, 12 hour video game analysis videos and stand-in for television formats. Bullish 24/7 streaming and high volume short form.

> Long form takes up too much time without the audience interaction of streaming. It allows attention to wander in ways short form doesn't. It's the territory of millennials, the unemployed and toddlers.

## filters-monetization
- **Date:** 2026-06-17
- **Status URLs (in order):** https://x.com/pleometric/status/2067279690553782341 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2067282540780786113
- **Source:** [pleometric-longposts.md §36](../pleometric-longposts.md#36-filters-monetization)

> AI video is a meeting of cheap, scalable production with fine tuned distribution algos, fast remixing culture and broad cheap distribution a la tiktok

> The filters are already here! All short form platforms (and youtube) are starting to tag, push back and demonetize content they deem "below a certain threshold of quality". AI videos are not eligible for monetization on TT for example. I expect all platforms to follow suit until there is a bigger product drive for their own offerings e.g. VEO and SeeDance.
> We're now squarely in the race between filters and producers.
