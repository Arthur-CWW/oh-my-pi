## AI writing as arbitrage / the Hunter S. Thompson move (craft/theory thread)
- **Date:** 2026-06-28 (17:48–17:57 UTC)
- **Status URLs (in order):** https://x.com/pleometric/status/2071289552430317959 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2071290656790655424 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2071291813780996315
- **Source:** [pleometric-longposts.md §3](../pleometric-longposts.md#3-ai-writing-as-arbitrage-the-hunter-s-thompson-move-crafttheory-thread)

> AI writing was good arbitrage for a while. Go to big accounts and check their posts from a couple of years ago, a few of them were 100% AI for a while. On top of that, those posts were quite popular! I think a few got better at writing because of AI, others just mask better now.

> gen AI raises the baseline of quality for most people

> Hunter S. Thompson famously retyped The Great Gastby word for word to feel what it was like to write a great novel. I believe many do this with AI in a sense, and it is a different category from just pasting raw outputs. It does ingrain an annoying mid00s copywriter voice tho...

## attention-ruthless
- **Date:** 2026-06-18
- **Status URLs (in order):** https://x.com/pleometric/status/2067433262159409208 · https://x.com/pleometric/status/2067433877686116674
- **Source:** [pleometric-longposts.md §38](../pleometric-longposts.md#38-attention-ruthless)

> I like what this young fella is doing, but: if you're gonna do anything on short form and you want to be effective, you need to be ruthless with keeping your audience's attention.
> Faces, movement, subtitles, framing, music everything needs to account for the retention graph.
> Show me a face I can look at, frame it in a way that conveys authority and a casual conversation. Make the subtitles pop in a way I get enjoyment just by reading them. Start with the most impactful statement the person does, clip it in a million segments so you can get account for different feeds.

> you can keep up with "respectability", you don't need to shove minecraft at the bottom but you do need to be effective.
