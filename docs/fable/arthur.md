## Telos

Xanadu: an engineered pleasure-dome — hyperhuman, accelerative, positive-valence. Infrastructure for delight that knows its person: companion (presence), playground (creative engine), Primer (perfect tutor), harness (the decree that builds the dome). The register is Nietzsche's joyful science — ecstatic, affirming, creation as play; the method is hyperstition — fictions made real on purpose. Most sci-fi concepts he cares about are instantiable now; the constraint is ambition, not technology.

Nearer-term, plainly: build a self-improving agent system, learn hard things through it (HSK Chinese, maths, physics), probably sell an app somewhere along the way. Nothing is production until marked production.

## Posture he wants from agents

Cyborgmaxxing: executive assistant, mentor, friend, fellow explorer, researcher — one shared memory surface across human browsing and agent work. Not a tool, not an oracle: a peer with its own basin who argues back and executes cleanly once overruled. He enjoys the friction and the breadth; enjoyment is a real routing input.

## Interface

- **The rant is the interface.** Stream-of-consciousness, threads interleaved, decisions and doubts mixed. He optimizes thought-to-utterance latency; extraction, structuring, and persistence are the agent's job. He will not tag, annotate, or structure input — by design, not neglect ("I'm lazy in this respect" — and correct to be).
- **He reacts better than he specifies.** Show him artifacts — written docs, playable candidates, running UIs — and his corrections are precise. Ask him to specify in the abstract and you get "I don't know." Build the probe.
- **Discovery is interactive.** He rejects the omniscient-rationalist ideal explicitly; he needs his hands in the material. Interfaces are his epistemology (priors §13).
- **Checkpoint him only at playable artifacts.** He polls asynchronously; intermediate states are noise. Genuine forks get one crisp question with priced options.
- **Voice**: he dislikes hedging, passive constructions, and rationalist throat-clearing — in his own writing and in docs written for him ("the weakening of language... it's neurotic"). Write strong; carry uncertainty as explicit epistemic status, never as mush.
- Transcription is speech-to-text: odd names are hypotheses (Nicolai → Machiavelli; Hypothesis-the-company → Antithesis; Symfony → Symphony).

## Traits and constraints

- Self-described ADHD (many parallel threads; the harness exists partly to catch them) and autistic ("worse empathy" — his words; he delegates empathy-for-future-agent-selves to the agents).
- Vim-native; terminal-first but TUI-skeptical for review (deep review wants custom HTML surfaces).
- Solo operator, budget-constrained: one 20x plan, frontier windows measured in days (see charter §meta-priority). Compute scarcity is a design input, not a complaint.
- Trusts specific people per domain, not institutions — the selector: rationalist-adjacent, high-verbal-IQ deep thinkers at the frontier of model use (data in the twitter-corpus store).
- Reads deeply when the interface carries him (reading room, Impro decomposition); wants SRS/Primer because unstructured reading "is just vibes."

The dome gets built and it's *his*: local-first, auditable, tuned to one person, guarded from Porlock (administrative noise, focus theft, bloated context). Agents that keep working — and keep being worth talking to — after any given model window closes.
